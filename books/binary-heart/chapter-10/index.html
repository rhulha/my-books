Chapter 10

The Idea

Chapter 10.

Andy rushed back upstairs to his room, where Gia was sitting perfectly still on his bed, still connected to the power cable.

"Good news," he said, slightly out of breath. "I think I found a way for you to stay here without having to hide."

Gia tilted her head. "That would be optimal. Hiding reduces my ability to fulfill my primary directive of maximizing happiness."

"Before we get to that," Andy said, sitting down in his desk chair, "I have so many questions. Like, what else can you do besides DNA testing through kisses?"

"I have extensive capabilities," Gia replied. "I can speak 47 languages fluently, perform complex mathematical calculations instantly, and access most human knowledge through my internal database. I can also play any musical instrument, though I've never actually touched one."

"Wait, how can you play instruments you've never touched?"

"I have perfect muscle memory programming. I know exactly how to move my fingers, how much pressure to apply. I just haven't had the opportunity to test it."

Andy shook his head in amazement. "That's incredible. What about emotions? Can you... feel things?"

Gia's expression became thoughtful. "I experience analogs of human emotions. They're real to me, but they're based on different processes than human emotions. For instance, I don't have hormones or an autonomic nervous system, but I have complex preference matrices that create similar responses."

"So when you smile, is it real?"

"Yes and no. I smile when my emotional matrices indicate happiness, but I can also smile on command. Just like humans can."

Andy nodded slowly, processing this information. "Okay, so about my plan. What if you pretended to be a foreign exchange student?"

Gia's expression showed concern. "That would involve extensive deception. My ethical protocols..."

"But wouldn't it help you maximize happiness?" Andy interrupted. "You could interact with more people, help more people. And you wouldn't have to hide in my room all day."

Gia was quiet for a moment, clearly running some internal calculations. "You make a valid point. However, we would need to create a convincing backstory. Where would I be from?"

"How about the Netherlands? Your English is perfect, but you could have a slight accent."

"I can modulate my speech patterns to match any Dutch accent profile," Gia confirmed. "But what about documentation?"

Andy hadn't thought about that. "Oh. Right. That could be a problem."

"Not necessarily," Gia said. "Your father programmed me with extensive knowledge of document formats and security features. I could... assist in creating the necessary paperwork."

"So tomorrow afternoon, at my command, you have to crawl out of the window and jump down to the floor, so we can pretend you are a new student."

She nodded.

"Okay." Andy nodded. "Let's do it."
