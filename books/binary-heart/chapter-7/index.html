Chapter 7

The School

Chapter 7.

The night had been crazy. For the first time in his life, he slept next to a woman in his bed.
It wasn't a real woman, of course, but it was a two billion-dollar android who could pass for a human.
The warmth of Gia felt incredibly real and he couldn't help but feel a magnetic pull.
Eventually he fell asleep and from that point on he slept like a baby.
The next morning, he woke up to the smell of pancakes wafting through the air.
He stretched and rubbed his eyes, still half-asleep.
Then he checked. Yes, she was still there, lying next to him, her eyes closed, pretending to breathe.

"Andy!" called his mother from downstairs. "Breakfast is ready!"
He groaned and rolled over, trying to ignore the world for just a few more seconds.
"Thank you, I'm coming!" he shouted back.

"Gia," he whispered, nudging her gently. "Can you hear me?"

She stirred slightly, her eyes fluttering open. Then she turned to him, her expression softening as she smiled.

"Good morning, Andy," she said.

"Good morning, did you... uhm, sleep well?"

"I did," she replied, her voice warm and soothing. "I'm 100 percent charged now."

"Okay, great. Listen, I have to go to school now. Can you stay hidden in my room until I get back?"

"Sure."

"I don't think my mom will come in if my bed is made. So just stay under the covers. I will place a pillow next to you to hide your shape a bit."

"Okay, I will stay here until you return."

"Okay, thank you!" Andy was a bit surprised how easy it was to talk to her.

He got up and quickly dressed, throwing on a hoodie and jeans before heading downstairs.

The smell of pancakes filled the air, and he couldn't help but feel a pang of hunger.

His mom was in the kitchen, flipping pancakes on the griddle.

"Morning, sleepyhead," she said with a smile. "I made your favorite."
"Thanks, Mom," he said, grabbing a plate and piling it high with pancakes.

"Did you sleep well last night?" she asked, raising an eyebrow.

Andy coughed, nearly choking on a mouthful of pancake. "Uh, yeah. Just... you know, tired from studying."
"I bet," she said, her tone teasing.

Andy finished his breakfast quickly; he looked at his watch and said, "Thanks Mom, you're the best!"

He grabbed his backpack and headed for the door.

"Have fun dear!" his mom called after him, just before the door closed behind him.

He threw his backpack into the backseat of his Ford Fiesta and hopped in.

Usually he cranked up the music, but today he was too distracted to even think about it.

How was he supposed to go to school like nothing happened, when probably the most advanced robot in the world was lying under his blanket?

He drove to school, his mind racing with thoughts of Gia and the night they had spent together.

Andy already knew that today would be a slog.

He would count the seconds until the last bell rang and he could go back home to Gia.

He parked his car in the lot and stepped out, taking a deep breath of the crisp morning air.

Andy could see the school building looming ahead, a mix of brick and glass that felt both familiar and daunting.

He passed the other students milling about, some laughing and chatting, others rushing to their classes.

The doors to Riverside High were both wide open at this time, and he stepped inside, the sound of chatter and laughter filling the air.

Andy's sneakers squeaked against the linoleum floor of the school's main hallway.

He kept his head down, trying to blend into the crowd of students rushing to their classes.

He made a quick stop at his locker, fumbling with the combination lock before grabbing his physics books.

"Hey, Thompson!" Ryan Carter's voice cut through the noise. "I heard you had a hot date last night."

Andy's heart skipped a beat, but he kept his eyes on his locker.
Oohs and aahs erupted from the group of popular kids surrounding Ryan.
Andy could feel their eyes on him, waiting for a reaction. He turned around and faced them.
Ryan was grinning, his friends snickering behind him.
"Hi Ryan," said Andy, trying to act cool.
"Yeah, what's her name? Aiusha?"

Andy felt a wave of relief wash over him. They were just talking about Dota.

"Her name is Aiushtha the Enchantress and yes, I played with her last night," Andy said with confidence.

Ryan's grin widened. "Sure, sure. We all know you're a pro at playing with your little fairies."
The group erupted in laughter as Andy slammed his locker shut.
Andy felt his face flush with anger, but he was lucky because his next class was in the opposite direction of Ryan and his minions.
He turned around and walked away, trying to ignore the laughter echoing behind him.

Andy slipped into his AP Physics class, dropping into his usual seat near the back.
The burning of his face started to ease as he pulled out his textbook.

"Don't let them get to you," whispered Tom, his best friend, sliding into the seat next to him.

"They're just jealous because you're actually going somewhere after graduation," he added, nudging Andy with his elbow.

"I'm not sure that's how it works, Tom," Andy muttered, flipping through the pages of his textbook.

"I'll buy lunch today, how does that sound?" Tom said.

Andy looked up, "Thanks, Tom, that's really nice of you."

Tom smiled. "Just don't let this stupid jerk get to you. He's just a spoiled rich kid with too much time on his hands."

"Yeah, I know," Andy said, trying to shake off the lingering embarrassment, just as the teacher walked in.

Economics...

One Mississippi, two Mississippi… this would be a long, long day. Four Mississippi.
