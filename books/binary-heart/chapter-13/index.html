Chapter 13

The Break

Chapter 13.

During lunch, Ryan Carter and his crew constantly eyed them.
They were sitting at a table in the corner of the cafeteria, away from the crowd.
Gia stabbed at her salad, but not really eating anything, while Andy was eating a sandwich.

Ryan suddenly stood up and started to walk towards them.
Andy tensed up, but Gia just looked at him.
"Hello Ryan," said Andy, frostily.
"Hi Andy, may I ask you to introduce me to your friend?"
"Ryan, this is Gia. Gia, this is Ryan."
"Hello Ryan, nice to meet you!" said Gia, extending her hand.
Ryan shook it. "Hello Gia, it's a pleasure to meet you. Welcome to the US of A. Your English is perfect, if I may say so."
There was just the slightest awkward moment, when Ryan kept her hand just a little bit too long.
But maybe Andy was just imagining things.
"Thank you," said Gia, pulling her hand back.

"May I join you?" asked Ryan.
Gia seemed to be about to say something, but Andy beat her to it. "We were just about to leave."
"It will only take a moment," said Ryan.
"Fine," said Andy with a sigh, "what do you want?"

"I am having a little get-together at my place on Saturday. I would be honored if you would like to join us."
Ryan looked at Gia, as he extended the invitation and Andy wasn't sure if he was included.

"Sorry, I'm spending Saturday with Andy," said Gia.
Andy was unable to keep back his smile.
Ryan looked at Andy with disbelief. "This Andy?"
Gia nodded and Andy's smile disappeared. "Hey asshole!" said Andy, now standing up.
Andy was surprised by his own behavior; if things escalated, he wouldn't stand a chance.
Ryan looked at him and extended his hands. "Relax my friend. I'm sorry, okay? Of course you both are invited."
The adrenaline in Andy's system started to recede, surprised by Ryan's reaction.
"Uhm, thank you," said Andy. "We'll think about it."
Ryan snapped his fingers and looked at Andy. "Be there or be square."
Then he turned to Gia with a smile, "I hope you can make it."
He waved her goodbye before turning around and walking back to his table where his friends were waiting with excitement.

Andy sat back down, looking at Gia.
"What?" asked Gia.
"Would you like to go to this party?"
Gia looked at him, "I don't know. Do you want to go?"
Andy shrugged, "I don't know. I've never been invited to one of the cool parties before."
Gia laughed, her smile mesmerizing Andy.
Andy picked up the silverware. "We have separate classes now, let's go."

Andy went to advanced computer programming, while Gia went to advanced literature.

Andy kept checking his phone. He had given her his old phone and a prepaid SIM card.
Everything seemed to be fine, as he read her last message:

"Mrs. Solano asked about Shakespeare's use of dramatic irony. I referenced three examples from Act II of Macbeth, maintaining appropriate human-level analytical skills."
Andy chuckled and sent a thumbs-up back before going back to pretending to concentrate on the computer programming lesson.
The subject matter of his class was stuff he had done when he was 12.
