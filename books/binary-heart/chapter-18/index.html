Chapter 18

The Party

Chapter 18.

Andy had to park his Ford Fiesta two streets over, the only available spot he could find.

He glanced at Gia. His mother had put on some make-up on Gia.
Her eyes and lips were even bigger now and more pronounced.
Then he looked at Tom, his face pale in the dim light, yet very excited.

"We can still turn around," he said, watching another group of students walking towards Ryan's house.

"No way, José," Tom said, "Let's check it out; if Ryan is a jerk we can still leave."

"Fine," Andy sighed and unbuckled his seatbelt.

They walked the streets without a word, the music growing louder with each step.

Andy felt a strange sense of unease, as if he were watching himself from a distance.

Then they saw the Carters' house, a huge three-story stone building with a porch and a large front yard.

The bass throbbed, vibrating the windows and making Andy's chest tighten with each beat.

He'd never been to a party like this before. The idea of being surrounded by so many people and so much noise made his stomach churn.

Red Solo cups littered the front lawn, glowing under the porch light like bizarre garden ornaments.

Groups of teenagers clustered around the entrance, their laughter mixing with the music.

Earlier they had gone to a shopping mall and bought Gia a dress.
It was a simple black dress, but it fit her perfectly, accentuating her slim figure.
Andy had made sure not to buy anything too revealing, but the dress still made him feel uncomfortable.
Gia, on the other hand, seemed thrilled with the purchase, twirling in front of the mirror and smiling at her reflection.

Andy let Gia go first, following her up the steps to the porch and Tom behind him.
A couple of people looked at Gia and then at Andy and shook their heads.
Andy knew exactly what they were thinking.

Inside, the party pulsed with energy. Bodies pressed together in the living room, moving to Montell Jordan's 'This Is How We Do It'. The air felt thick with sweat and cheap cologne.

Andy recognized faces from school, but everyone looked different in the neon light, more grown up somehow. They pushed their way through the crowd, looking for Ryan.

Andy figured they should at least thank their host, even if the invitation hadn't been entirely genuine. But Ryan was nowhere to be found.

Tom started to jump up and down to the rhythm of the music, waving his arms in the air. "This is awesome!"

"Come, let's dance," shouted Tom, grabbing Gia's hand. Gia looked at Andy, who nodded. "Knock yourself out," he said, not interested in participating himself.

Gia smiled and let Tom lead her into the crowd. Andy watched them go.

He spotted a couch in the corner and made his way towards it.
Two girls were already sitting there. They looked at him and smiled.

"Hello," he shouted over the music. "May I sit next to you?"

"Yes, of course," said one of them, bopping her head to the beat.
"Yes, but only if you bring us drinks," said the other, grinning.

Andy sighed and looked at the kitchen. It was like a jungle in there.
He made his way through the crowd, trying to avoid spilling anyone's drink.
After what felt like eternity, he finally reached the kitchen.
He took a bottle of vodka and a jug of fruit punch from the counter and poured two cups.
Andy guessed the ratio was about 1 to 3, but he wasn't sure.
He took both cups and extended his elbows slightly to avoid spilling.
He fought his way back through the kitchen, through the living room and back to the couch.
"Here you go ladies," he said, handing them the cups.
"Thanks," they said in unison and took the cups.
"What's your name?" asked the girl on the left.
"Andy," he shouted.
"Hi Andy, I'm Daisy," said the left one. "I'm Lilly," said the right one.
"Nice to meet you," Andy said, smiling before sitting down.
The two girls bumped their cups together and took a sip.
Lilly leaned over to Andy. "Hey Andy, did you forget the alcohol?" she shouted.
"I'm sorry, I didn't know how much to put in," Andy shouted back.
Daisy laughed. "It's okay, we'll survive."

Andy smiled and leaned back, watching the crowd.
Suddenly he saw Tom, but Gia was missing.
He looked around, but she was nowhere to be found.
"Oh shit," exclaimed Andy, standing up.
"Where are you going?" asked Lilly.
"I have to find a friend," Andy shouted.
"We could be your friends," said Daisy, grinning.
But Andy didn't even process the words.

He pushed his way through the crowd towards Tom.
Tom was still dancing, completely lost in the music.
"Tom, where is Gia?" Andy shouted.
Tom looked at him, his eyes glazed. Then he recognized Andy.
"Andy!" he shouted back.
"Tom, where is Gia?" Andy shouted again.
"I don't know," Tom shouted back.
"What do you mean? You don't know." Andy shouted.
"I don't know where she is," Tom shouted back.
"Damn it Tom," Andy muttered and pushed his way through the crowd.
He walked around each room that had an open door and looked inside, scanning the face of every person.
Then he went for the door to the garden and went outside.
The air was much cooler and a blessing to breathe.
A couple of kids sat in plastic garden chairs, smoking and talking.
There was a pool in the garden, but nobody was swimming.
He saw a couple making out in the corner, but it wasn't Gia.
A group of kids played beer pong.
Andy walked around the pool. He saw nothing but a few empty cups.
There was a small pool house. Of course Ryan had a pool house.
Andy walked towards it and knocked on the door.
No answer.
He opened the door and looked inside.
It was a bit dark, but he immediately saw Ryan and Gia sitting on a large red-brown leather couch.
"Gia?" Andy said, stepping inside.
Gia looked up, "Hi Andy."
"Is everything okay?" Andy asked.
"Yes, everything is fine," Gia said.
Andy looked at Ryan, who looked very unhappy.
"What's up?" asked Andy.
Ryan sighed, "Not much."
Andy thought for a good thing to say to get Gia out of the pool house.
"Hey Gia, may I have the next dance?" Andy asked.
"Sure," Gia said and stood up. She turned to Ryan. "Please excuse me and thank you for the nice conversation."
Ryan nodded at Gia but didn't really say anything.
Gia went to Andy and he took her hand and led her out of the pool house.
"Holy cow, Gia. I think Ryan is trying to hit on you," Andy said.
"Well, we were just talking about the Netherlands," Gia said. "He seemed really interested in where I come from."
They walked around the pool with a respectful distance.
"Yeah I bet," said Andy. "Please be careful around him, I don't trust that guy."
Gia nodded and they went back inside, the thick air of the party hitting like a truck.
"So you promised me a dance," Gia said, smiling.
Andy sighed, "Trust me, you really don't want to see me dancing."
Gia laughed, "Oh come on, I'm sure you're a great dancer if you really try."
Andy smiled, "Whatever, I will try to copy Tom." He looked around, but couldn't see him.

"Andy! Gia!" Tom's voice cut through the music. He emerged from the kitchen, red cup in hand, grinning widely. "Are you ready to party?"

He threw his arms around Andy, sloshing his drink. The sharp smell of vodka mixed with fruit punch hit Andy's nose.

"Having fun?" Andy steadied his friend.

"So much fun!" Tom's words slurred slightly. He turned to Gia, his eyes bright. "Want to dance?"

"Yes please," Gia smiled, already moving her body to the beat.

Gia pulled Andy after her. The music was so loud it seemed to vibrate through his bones.
Tom and Gia danced and it looked so natural, so easy.
Andy gave it a try, but he felt like a fish out of water.
"You have to stop caring about what others may think."
Andy turned around at the new voice. It was Daisy.
"Follow my lead," she lifted her arms and started to move her hips from left to right.
Andy tried to copy her movements, but he was still too stiff.
"Relax," she said, putting her hands on his hips.
She started to move his hips from left to right.
"That's it," she said, smiling.
"Exaggerate your movement, but try to keep up with the beat. That's it."
He had seen Daisy in school before, but never in his wildest dreams would he have thought that she would teach him how to dance.
"Where is Lilly?" he asked.
Daisy shrugged, "She's probably making out with some guy."
Andy turned a bit to the side, to check on Tom and Gia.
They were still dancing and Tom was getting a bit too close to Gia, for Andy's taste.
He turned back to Daisy, "Thank you for the dance lesson."
She smiled, "Wanna take the next step?"
Andy looked at her, "What do you mean?"
She embraced him and started to move her hips against his.
He tried to follow her lead, but he felt like a robot.
"You have to bend your knees a bit," she said, bending her own knees as an example.
Andy bent his knees and now really tried to move his hips to complement her movement.
Daisy was visibly impressed, "Hey, you're doing great!" she shouted over the music.
Andy smiled, feeling a bit more confident. This wasn't so bad after all.
He turned to the side again, checking in on Tom and Gia.
They had copied Daisy's and Andy's movements and were now dancing very close together.
Andy felt a pang of jealousy.
"Who are you looking at?" asked Daisy.
He turned back to her. "I'm sorry, nobody."
Daisy smiled. "You're a terrible liar. Is she your girlfriend?"
Andy tilted his head. "It's complicated."
Daisy nodded. "I heard that before."
They danced for a while, but then the music changed.
"I need a break," Andy shouted. "Me too, wanna get some fresh air?" Daisy shouted back.
Andy checked on Tom and Gia, but they were still dancing.
Andy lifted one finger at Daisy and she nodded.
Andy moved closer to Tom and Gia, "Hey you two dance freaks, can I leave you two alone for a moment?"
Tom looked at Andy. "Sure, go ahead."
"Can I trust you to take care of Gia?" Andy asked.
"Of course," Tom said, grinning, giving a high five to Andy.
Andy nodded and he turned back around to Daisy.
"Let's go," he shouted and they made their way through the crowd to the door.
The air outside again felt like a cool breeze.
Daisy spotted two garden chairs at the far side of the pool.
"Wanna sit over there?" she said.
Andy nodded and they made their way to the chairs.
They sat down and Andy took a deep breath.
"Thanks for the dance lesson," he said.
Daisy smiled, "You're welcome. You're a fast learner."
He smiled, "I'm not so sure about that."
Daisy laughed, "Trust me, you're doing great."
They sat in silence for a moment, watching the stars above.
Then Daisy turned to Andy, "So, what's the deal with you and Gia?"
Andy sighed, "I don't know. It's complicated."
Daisy raised an eyebrow, "She is an exchange student, right?"
He nodded, "Yes, from the Netherlands."
"So she is living with you?" Daisy asked.
Andy nodded.
Daisy leaned back in her chair, "You're holding hands. That usually means something."
"Yeah, but it started as a prank on Ryan."
Daisy laughed, "A prank on Ryan? That's actually hilarious. I can tell you for a fact that it worked."
He smiled.
Daisy looked at him, "You say it started as a prank. What is it now?"
Andy made a decision. "Look Daisy, I will be honest with you. I've seen you a couple of times in school. You are very beautiful, but I think you have a boyfriend, right?"
"Had a boyfriend," Daisy corrected him.
He raised an eyebrow, "What do you mean?"
Daisy sighed, "We broke up a couple of weeks ago. He was a jerk."
Andy nodded, "I'm sorry to hear that. Okay, well, you are very beautiful and I would like to get to know you better. But is there a realistic chance that we could become more than just friends? I mean tomorrow as well, when your blood alcohol level is back to normal."
Daisy laughed, "You're very direct. But that's okay, I'm not looking for a boyfriend only for a crazy night with someone."

Andy swallowed hard and thought about the cliché that men in a relationship were more attractive to other women.
He knew that asking any further questions would be a huge mood killer.
Daisy looked at him, "So, what do you say?"
"Could I potentially bring Gia home first and then meet you again?"
"Isn't she an adult?" Daisy asked.
Andy sighed, "Yes, but I kinda have responsibility for Gia."
Daisy smiled, "Sure, I'm not going anywhere."

"Okay, let me take care of that first," Andy said and stood up. "Please don't go anywhere."
Daisy smiled, "I can't promise that."
Andy turned and made his way back to the house.

He found Tom and Gia still dancing, but Tom was all over Gia, ignoring her attempts to maintain distance.

"Come on Gia, dance with me," Tom insisted.

Gia tried to push him away, but Tom was too strong. When suddenly she saw Andy.

"Andy!" she called out, relief in her voice.

Tom's face darkened. "Andy doesn't mind sharing, right buddy?"

"Back off, Tom." Andy stepped between them.

"Oh, now you're protecting your precious girlfriend?" Tom's voice rose. "What's the matter, did Daisy turn you down?"

Andy's face hardened. "You're drunk, Tom. Let's get you some water."

"No!" Tom shoved Andy back. Andy stumbled, his feet tangling, and he hit the ground.

The party seemed to pause, dozens of eyes turning toward them.

Gia moved to help Andy up, but Tom grabbed her arm. "You and your stupid plastic girlfriend!"

"Shut up Tom!" Andy scrambled to his feet.

"What, you can't share a bit of plastic and wire with an old friend?" Tom's voice echoed through the suddenly quiet room.

"Shut up, you idiot!" Andy's hands balled into fists.

"You're the idiot!" Tom's grip tightened on Gia's arm.

"Come on, Gia. Let's get out of here." Andy reached for her.

"So now you will have two girlfriends and not even let me have one?" Tom yanked Gia closer. "A great friend you are."

Andy didn't think. He just moved, shoving Tom hard enough to break his grip on Gia.

Tom stumbled back, then launched himself forward, pushing Andy with both hands.

Andy felt something snap inside him. All the fear, all the tension of the past weeks, crystallized into a single moment of clarity.

He pulled back his arm and punched Tom square in the face, right as someone turned off the music.

The crack of knuckles against jaw echoed through the silent room.
