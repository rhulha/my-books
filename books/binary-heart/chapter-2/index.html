Chapter 2

The Game

Chapter 2.

Six months later.

"Come on guys, push mid!"

Andy stared at the 3D computer graphics of a fantasy forest cut through by a shallow river.

"Aegis is running out soon."

His hand flicked the mouse at seemingly random times, while his middle finger clicked the right mouse button in an endless stream.

Then his teammates appeared one by one at his side. The familiar rush of dominating a match coursed through his veins.

"Andy?" His mother's voice cut through the background.

He winced, then calmed himself. "Guys, I need a moment, please pause."

Then he disconnected from the game.

Andy took his headset off and turned around. "Hey Mom."

Using his fingers, he combed his shoulder-length brown hair back, trying to look presentable.

A smile tugged at his lips.

Andy loved his mother and took great care to show it.

He knew she was going through a tough time. They both were.

"Are you in the middle of something?" she asked.

Andy nodded. "Yeah, round of Dota. What's up?"

She hesitated, then said, "I finally decided to clean out your father's office. Can you help me?"

Andy's eyes widened, he knew what a big step this was for her.

"Of course! May I finish the round?" asked Andy, still surprised.

"20 Minutes?" she asked, familiar with his gaming habits.

Andy nodded. "20 minutes."

She smiled, "I'll make some tea."

Andy put his headset back on and reconnected to the game.

"Guys, we have 20 minutes. I'm six-slotted. Let's finish this."
