Chapter 12

The Class

Chapter 12.

Andy parked the Ford Fiesta his mother had bought him from the insurance money of his father's totaled car.
"This is it. Are you ready?" he asked Gia.
"I'm fully charged and ready," she replied.
"Remember, you are a foreign exchange student from the Netherlands. Don't say stuff like that when we are around others."
"Yes, I know," Gia confirmed.
"If you are in a situation where you are unsure how to react, just act annoyed. That's what all teenagers do."
Gia nodded.
"And if you get weird personal questions, just say that you are not comfortable talking about it."
"Got it."

He exited the car and walked around to open the door for her.
Gia wore a simple blue dress and carried a brown leather backpack. Her movements were fluid and natural.

She stepped out and looked at the school building. "It's big," she said.
Andy nodded. "Yeah, it can be a bit overwhelming at first, but you'll get used to it."
Andy remembered his first day at Riverside High and he could empathize with her.
"Okay, let's go," he said, taking her hand as they walked towards the entrance.

Getting her approved for school had been surprisingly easy.
Andy had expected more questions, but the secretary had her fill out a form,
looked at Gia's laminated fake Dutch ID, her fake immunization records,
typed a few things into the computer and then handed her a schedule with double-optioned classes.
Meaning some classes could be taken in different time slots.

"Welcome to Riverside High, Gia," she had said with a smile.
It was for only one semester and Andy really didn't know what he would do with her after that, but he would figure it out when the time came.
Maybe she could even work in a Starbucks and afford her own apartment. He would have to look into that.

The closer they got to the entrance, the more students stared at them.
Gia's hand was warm and soft in his. He couldn't wait to see the look on Ryan Carter's face.
He felt a shiver run down his spine. This was easily the craziest thing he had ever done.

Andy opened Riverside High's front doors and let Gia walk in first.
Immediately heads started to turn in their direction and people started to whisper.

They crossed the hallway and turned right, going for Andy's locker.
And there he was, Ryan Carter, talking to his girlfriend, encircled by a couple of his minions.
Andy's hand tightened around Gia's. Then one of the minions saw them. Ryan noticed his friend staring and turned around.
His eyes widened and his jaw dropped. His girlfriend also followed his gaze and her lips quickly pressed into a tight frown.
Ryan didn't compose himself fast enough, and his girl elbowed him in the ribs.
"Ouch," he said, rubbing his side and closing his mouth.
Andy laughed and turned towards his locker. Ryan's eyes darted between Andy and Gia.
Andy picked up his books and closed the locker with a bit more force than necessary.

"First period's Chemistry," Andy said, leading Gia past a shell-shocked Ryan and through the rest of the crowded hallway.
Gia squeezed his hand. "This is fun!"
"I'm glad you like it!" Andy said, not even trying to keep his voice down.

"I've reviewed the entire AP Chemistry curriculum," Gia announced. "I'll maintain a 94% accuracy rate on tests to avoid suspicion."
Andy laughed, "Better aim for 84%, we don't need any more attention."
"Understood," she said, her eyes twinkling.

Andy looked at her, no way this was a robot. Except, of course, for the fact that she held his hand.
Last night, when he was lying in bed, failing to fall asleep, he had thought about the implications of Gia's existence.
What if almost all jobs could be done by robots? What would that mean for society?
At 2 billion a pop the GX-7 series was not exactly affordable, but as Gia had pointed out,
the cost per unit quickly went down the more that get produced.
Was that the reason his father had been so secretive about his work?

They entered the classroom and Andy saw two seats in the back.
He led Gia there and they sat down.
Their remaining classmates trickled in as well, all staring at Gia, news had spread fast.

Mr. Anderson walked in and the class fell silent.
He was the only one not staring at Gia for long and he had a piece of paper in his hand.

"Good morning class. I see that we have a new student joining us for the semester. Please welcome Gia from the Netherlands."
He placed the info sheet on his desk and started to clap his hands. The students followed suit, some more enthusiastic than others.

"Welkom in Amerika, Gia. Ik hoop dat je plezier hebt," Mr. Anderson said in fluent Dutch, surprising the class.
Andy's heart skipped a beat. Why the hell did he know Dutch? Cold sweat started to form on his forehead.
He had talked about everything with Gia, except for this.

Gia smiled and replied, "Dank je, meneer Anderson. Ik zal mijn best doen."

Andy felt like Neo dodging a bullet in slow motion.
His heart slowly started to pick up pace again and he wiped his forehead and blew out some air.

Class was going well after that.
Gia raised her hand exactly three times - her answers were correct, but not exhaustively detailed.

Still Mr. Anderson was impressed. "See guys, this is what education in Europe is like. They actually learn stuff."
The class moaned in unison and just when Mr. Anderson wanted to say something in response the bell saved everyone from further embarrassment.

Andy packed his backpack and a few students approached Gia.
She was polite and diplomatically answered every question.
Andy was proud of her. She was doing much better than he had thought.

Suddenly Tom jogged into the classroom and went straight to Andy.
"Andy, what the fuck, dude!"
Andy smiled, "What's up Tom?"
"What's up? What's up? Everyone is talking about you; that you brought a hot girl to school and that she is holding your hand."
Tom looked at Gia, then back at Andy, then back at Gia, then back at Andy again. "Is that her?"
"Tom, don't be rude," scolded Andy, pretending to be serious though he was beaming with pride.

"Sorry," Tom said, then he turned towards Gia, "Hello, my name is Thomas, but please call me Tom."
He extended his hand. Gia shook it, "Hello Tom, it's a pleasure to meet you, my name is Gia."
"Gia, I can assure you, the pleasure is all mine." He turned back to Andy. "So Andy, what the fuck?"
Gia chuckled.
"Relax Tom, she is a foreign exchange student from the Netherlands."
"So you held hands as a prank or what?"
Andy leaned his head to the left. He hadn't really thought of what to tell Tom about all of this.
"Well you know, in Europe things are way more relaxed about these things, you know?"
"What the fuck?"
"In Europe friends can hold hands," he explained.
"That's fucking bullshit dude."
"Tom, there is a lady..."
Tom looked at Gia again, visibly shocked, "Sorry, Ma'am"
Gia nodded with a smile. "We might be more than just friends." She winked at Tom.
Andy sighed.
Tom jumped up and down. Then he turned towards Gia again.
"Holy hell, please don't mind my saying, but why Andy?"
"Tom, I'm right here!" protested Andy.
"I think he's cute," announced Gia with a continued smile.
"Holy cow, so you settled for the first man you met in America? Is that right?"
"Tom, seriously, I'm right here." Andy kicked him playfully from under the desk.
"Sorry, I need to know," Tom stared at Gia.
"I feel like I simply got extremely lucky," said Gia.
Tom shook his head, repeating her words in disbelief, "You got extremely lucky?"
Andy couldn't help but smile, "See, she feels lucky and it would be nice if my best friend could start to express something else than shock right now."
Tom shook his head. "Wow, wow, wow. You don't happen to have a sister, do you?"
"I do actually."
Andy was surprised to hear that. He had told her not to make her backstory to complicated.
"Is she of age?" asked Tom.
Andy kicked him again. "Dude!"
"Sorry, sorry, sorry. I'm just curious."
Gia smiled, "She is."
Tom's eyes lit up, "Really?"
"Yes, really. We are actually the same age."
"So you are twins?"
Gia tilted her head, "Hm, I guess you could say that, yes."
"What's her name?"
"Her name is Sarah."
Andy raised his eyebrows and took a couple of mental notes.
This was the first time he heard all of this as well.

"Sarah," Tom closed his eyes while he said her name. "Is she coming to America too?"
"Not specifically, no."
"Damn. Well, should that ever change, please let me be the first man to meet her, yeah?"
Gia started to laugh, "Okay, sure."
Andy shook his head, "Tom, you are unbelievable."
