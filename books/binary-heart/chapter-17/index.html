Chapter 17

The Invitation

Chapter 17.

"So Tom, how is the new semester going?" Debby asked.

Tom shrugged. "Pretty good, Mrs. Thompson. I really enjoy the new computer science classes they are now offering. It's a walk in the park for me and Andy."

"Have you decided on universities yet?" Debby inquired. "You two still want to go to the same one?"

"Yes, Mrs. Thompson, that's the plan. We are both looking at the same universities," Tom replied.

"Is MIT still your first choice?" Debby wondered aloud.

"Yes, but we are also looking at Stanford and Caltech." Andy interjected.

"So, what are the chances that both of you will be accepted to the same university?" Debby asked.

"We actually did the math on that," Tom said.

Andy nodded. "The individual acceptance rate at those three is around 4%. The chance of both of us getting accepted is 0.16%."

Tom continued. "So getting into our top 3 candidates is around 0.5%."

"0.48%" Gia corrected.

Everyone fell silent and looked at Gia.

Tom looked at Andy. "She's right. We talked about it earlier," said Andy quickly.

Debby looked at Gia. "I hear you are doing pretty well in class. Do you have any plans?"

Gia shrugged. "I'm not sure yet. I'm still exploring my options."

"Do you have a favorite subject?" Debby asked.

Gia shook her head. "Not really. I like math and science."

"Good for you." Debby smiled. "That field definitely could use more of a female touch."

"I agree, maybe you could join us, Gia." Tom said.

Gia looked at Andy. "I'll think about it."

They ate in silence for a few minutes, then Debby picked up the conversation again.

"So what about that party?" asked Debby, before eating a piece of chicken with gravy.

Andy looked up from his plate. "Which party?"

"The one at Ryan's place." She set her fork down. "Mrs. Carter told me all about it at the grocery store today."

Andy sighed. That stupid grocery store.

Andy shot a glance at Gia, who was methodically moving salad around her plate.

"I'm not really into parties," Andy said, pushing his peas into a neat line.

"I think it could be good for you." Debby reached for her wine glass. "You haven't been out in a while."

"Ryan hasn't exactly been nice to me in the past."

"Oh come on, you act like he's bullying you or something." Debby took a sip.

Andy stabbed a piece of chicken. "Yeah well..."

Debby tried changing the topic. "Mrs. Carter said Gia's quite the sensation at school."

Andy nodded. "That's exactly why he invited us. He just wants Gia at his party."

"Oh come on, Andy - now you're being paranoid."

"Mom, I'm not being paranoid."

Andy chewed his chicken, avoiding his mother's gaze.

"Wouldn't it be nice to get out of the house for a change?" Debby pressed. "Tom, Gia, what do you think?"

Gia looked at Tom, who shrugged. "I think it could be fun. I actually thought about going."

Andy shot a puzzled Tom a look. "Don't tell me Ryan invited you."

"Not exactly," Tom said. "But if I go with you guys, he will probably let me in."

Andy sighed. "Great."

Gia looked up from her plate. "I would like to learn what an American party is like."

"Ah, great, three against one," Andy muttered.

"Come on, Andy, don't take it like this," Debby said. "Why can't you just go and have fun?"

Andy sighed. "Fine, we'll go, but if Ryan starts hitting on Gia or is being a jerk, we're out of there. Agreed?"

Andy looked at Gia and Tom, who both nodded eagerly.

Debby raised her wine glass. "To democracy!"

Tom and Gia raised their glasses. "To democracy!"

Andy palmed his face with his right hand. "To democracy."
