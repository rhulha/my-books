Chapter 11

The Student

Chapter 11.

Andy rang the doorbell, his heart pounding in his chest.
He had a key, of course, but he wanted to give his mother a heads-up.
She opened the door, a curious look on her face.
"Andy," she said. Then her gaze fell on Gia. "And a girl."
"Hello, Mrs. Thompson," Gia said, making a little curtsy. "My name is Gia."
Debby looked in shock.
"Can we come in?" Andy asked.
Debby stepped aside, still looking at Gia like a zoo animal.
Andy motioned for Gia to enter first. She smiled at him and walked in. Andy followed.

Debby closed the door behind them.
"Andy, I need to talk to you in private," Debby said with a stern voice.
Andy nodded. He knew this would be coming.
He looked at Gia apologetically. "Just a second, we'll be right back."
Gia nodded, folded her hands and started to look around the living room.

Meanwhile Andy followed Debby into the kitchen.
Debby turned around and whispered to Andy, "What in the world is a girl doing here?"
"You said we can house a student," whispered Andy back.

"Yes, a male student," Debby hissed.
"You didn't say that."
"Of course I did."
"No, you didn't," retorted Andy.
"Well, it was implied."
Andy sighed. "Well, apparently not."
Debby looked at him, then at Gia. "You can't have a girl stay here."
"Why not?"
"Because I'm not stupid."
"Mom, she's just a student. And by the way, look at her, she's way out of my league. She's probably looking for college guys."
Debby looked at Gia again, then at Andy. Her eyes softened.
"Oh Andy, don't sell yourself so short."
"Mom, that's not the topic right now!"
"I think you're a great guy, anyone would be lucky..."
"Mom, focus please."
Debby looked at Gia again, then back at Andy.
She loved her son, but she knew he was right.
Debby sighed. "Okay fine, she can stay."
Andy wasn't sure if he should be happy or offended by her quick agreement after looking at them both.

"But the door stays open when she's in your room," she added, her expression leaving no room for compromise.
"Mom!" Andy protested, trying to sound appropriately scandalized while hiding his grin.
"Those are my terms." Debby said, extending her hand.
Andy pretended to think about it before quickly shaking her hand. "Deal!"
Debby shook his hand, way less enthusiastically.
Andy noticed this and turned the handshake into a hug; he truly loved the relationship he had with his mother.
She was simply the best. This was a great moment to show her how much he appreciated her.
"I love you, Mom!" Andy said. "I love you too, Andy," Debby said, hugging him back. "Now go back to your guest."
Andy turned around with the biggest smile and walked back to the living room, where Gia was looking at the family pictures on the wall.
"Let me show you your room," Andy said, leading her upstairs.

Now he just had to figure out how to get Gia enrolled in school, but that was tomorrow's problem.
