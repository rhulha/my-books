Chapter 20

The Advance

Chapter 20.

The next few days passed in a haze of tension. Ryan seemed to appear everywhere - lingering by Gia's locker, sitting near them at lunch, watching them in the parking lot after school. His presence felt like a storm cloud, growing darker with each passing day.

Tom had reappeared at school, but he avoided Andy like the plague. Their usual banter was replaced by awkward silences and furtive glances. Andy tried to approach him several times, but Tom always found a way to slip away.

"Hey beautiful," Ryan's voice echoed through the empty hallway.

Gia was gathering her books after staying late for a study group. "Need some company?"

"Not really. But thank you, Ryan." Gia's voice remained steady as she closed her locker.

Ryan stepped closer, blocking her path. "Come on, don't be like that."

His fingers reached for her face. "I could take such good care of you."

Gia stepped back. "I need to go. Andy's waiting." She looked for help, but incredibly, the hallway was empty.
She had never seen an empty hallway at this time of the day before.

"Andy?" Ryan laughed, the sound bouncing off the metal lockers. "You're a masterpiece and he is a loser. He doesn't deserve you."

He advanced, backing her against the lockers. "You need someone who can appreciate you for who you really are."

"Please let me go." Gia's voice remained calm, but her eyes darted toward the exit.

Ryan's hand slammed against the locker beside her head. "Don't you get it? You don't have a choice anymore."

He leaned in, his breath hot against her face. "You're mine now."

Gia's hand shot out, palm connecting with his chest. "No, I'm not."

Ryan stumbled back, surprise flickering across his face.

"Get away from me." Gia's voice was high-pitched and loud. "Andy!" she screamed.

They had planned to meet at the parking lot, but sometimes Andy surprised her at her locker. She hoped this was one of those times.

Ryan's other hand grabbed her arm, fingers digging into her synthetic skin. He tried to kiss her.

Gia twisted, breaking his grip with strength that surprised him.

"Listen to me! Either you stop resisting or I will tell the whole world that you are a robot!" Ryan's voice was a low growl.

Gia's eyes widened.
Andy had told her over and over again that no one could know.
She had to protect him. She had to maximize his happiness.

She stopped resisting.

"Good girl," Ryan leaned in and kissed her.

The kiss was rough and forceful, his hands grabbing at her body. Gia's mind raced, trying to find a way out of this nightmare.

Then, the door to the hallway burst open.

Andy stood there, his eyes wide with shock. Then he started to run, his heart pounding in his chest.

He ran faster than he had ever run before. He built more momentum than he had ever built before.

He didn't care about anything else in this world than to destroy Ryan Carter.

Ryan jerked back from Gia, eyes widening as he saw Andy charging toward him.

He quickly clenched his hands into fists, but it was too late.

Andy reached him in two seconds, his right fist connecting with Ryan's jaw with a sickening crack.

For a moment, Ryan's eyes rolled back in his head, then he crumpled to the ground, barely conscious.

Andy took Gia's hand, his touch warm and soft. "Are you okay?"

Gia nodded, her eyes wide with shock. "I'm fine."

Andy's hand was shaking as he reached for his phone. "I'm calling the police."

Gia's hand stopped him. "No, Andy. We can't."

"Why not?" Andy's voice was tight with anger.

Gia's eyes flicked to Ryan's crumpled form. "Because if they find out, they'll take me away from you."

Pain shot through Andy's heart at the thought. He stuffed his phone away.

Ryan carefully pushed himself into a sitting position, blood trickling from his split lip.
He slowly came back to his senses and spat out some blood.

His eyes locked onto Andy's. "That was a mistake, Andy." He wiped his mouth with the back of his hand.
"That was a big mistake."

"Come," Andy said, his voice low. "We're leaving." He took her hand into his.

Gia nodded, her movements jerky.
They went the other way, even if the path to the parking lot was longer.

Andy tried to pull out his keys, but his hand was burning with pain.
"Are you okay?" Gia asked.
"I think I broke my hand." Andy's voice was strained. "Can you get the car keys out of my pocket?"
She stepped closer, her hand brushing against his. "Do you think you can drive?"
"I don't know. I'll try. Can you drive a car?" Andy's voice was tight with pain.

Before she could answer, footsteps pounded against asphalt.
A cold shiver ran down Andy's spine. His worst nightmare was quickly approaching.
Ryan ran towards them, his letterman's jacket discarded, a baseball bat gripped in his hands.

Gia started to shake, her eyes wide with fear. "Andy, what do we do?"

Andy's heart pounded in his chest. "Get in the car and lock the door from the inside."

"Andy," she protested. "I won't leave you outside."

"Gia, I'm your primary commander!" He shouted. "Get in the car now!"

But it was too late. Ryan was upon them, his eyes wild with rage.

He pushed Andy to the side with the strength of a bull, sending him sprawling to the ground.

"Ryan, please," Andy shouted. Other students started to come closer and some started to take out their phones.

Ryan swung the bat in a wide arc. "You shouldn't have done that, Andy."

"I know, I'm sorry, Ryan. Please. I will do anything to make things right again."

Ryan's eyes were cold. "It's too late for that." Ryan swung the bat behind his back.

Gia closed her eyes. "I love you, Andy."

The bat whistled through the air, connecting with Gia's head with a sickening crack.

Andy's heart stopped beating. He couldn't breathe.

The sound wasn't the dull thud of wood against flesh.
Instead, it was sharp, crunchy - the sound of machinery breaking.

The top part of Gia's head split open and to the side,
revealing a mess of wires and metal components.

Her movements became jerky, she stumbled and lost her balance.

She fell to the right side, pushed by the impact, her right arm still reaching for Andy.

She crashed onto her side, her body convulsing, her legs trying to move into a position to stand back up again.

Ryan pulled the bat back, ready to strike again.

"Ryan. Please." Andy's voice was a whisper. "Please don't."
He wanted to die - die instead of her.

Ryan swung the bat again, this time connecting with Gia's chest.

The sound of breaking plastic echoed through the parking lot.
Everyone was filming the scene with their phones by now.
"Holy shit" and "What the hell?" echoed through the crowd.
"Jesus, Ryan, what the hell are you doing?" one even said, but it was too late.

Andy crawled forward and pushed his body over hers. He didn't know where the strength came from.
He didn't feel his pulse, just the electro-nuclear storm in his head.

"Get out of the way, Andy," Ryan's voice was cold.

Andy didn't move. "Go ahead, Ryan, kill me too."

Ryan looked at the scene before him.
Gia's body was twitching, her eyes flickering.
Wires spilled out of her like colorful spaghetti.
Circuit boards and metal components clattered to the ground.
A small servo motor rolled across the pavement, coming to rest at the feet of a stunned classmate.
Andy was lying on top of her protectively, his eyes closed. But it was too late.

Gia's remaining eye flickered once, twice, then stopped.
Her body swayed for a moment before stopping as well.

"Now everyone knows," Ryan said, dropping the bat.
It clattered against the asphalt, the sound echoing through the parking lot.
"Everyone knows what kind of freak you are."

The parking lot fell silent except for a few whispers.

Andy cradled Gia's broken form, his world crumbling around him as students began emerging from the school. News had spread like wildfire.

In the distance, a siren wailed.
