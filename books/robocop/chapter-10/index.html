Chapter 10: Sam the Park.

Saturday morning she drove to the park.

She stopped two blocks east, on a side street screened by a row of houses. She could not see the park from here. Neither could anyone in the park see her.

She opened the app and launched the drone from the back seat.

On her phone screen the camera showed rooftops, then treetops, the drone climbing in a wide arc away from the park. She guided it north, looping around behind the tree line on the far side.

The dealers were in their usual spot. She could see them on the feed as the drone descended through the canopy from behind. A large oak near the fence. She slowed the drone and eased it toward a thick branch about fifteen feet up.

The arm extended. The claw found the branch and closed.

The rotors spun down.

The feed steadied. Clear view of the corner. Clear view of the path.

She tapped record.

Then she set her phone on the passenger seat and pulled out into the street.
