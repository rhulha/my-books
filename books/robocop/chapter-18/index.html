Sam: The Briefcase

Sam - Chapter 18: The Briefcase.

Sam was parked three houses down, engine off, watching the house.

She had been there forty minutes.

The second recording had been different from the park footage — busier, more faces, more cars coming and going. But one guy had stood out.

White guy, maybe forty. Pressed shirt, sport coat. Sunglasses he never took off, not even once when he stepped out past midnight. He came twice in the three days of footage, always in a silver Audi, always carrying a leather briefcase.

She had pulled the two visits side by side.

On arrival the briefcase hung heavy, pulling his shoulder down slightly. On departure he swung it loose at his side.

She had ordered another tracker that same afternoon.

The silver Audi turned onto the street.

Sam watched it slow and pull into the driveway. The engine cut. The door opened.

Sport coat. Sunglasses.

He pulled the briefcase from the back seat, closed the car, and walked to the front door without looking around. The door opened before he knocked. Someone let him in.

Sam got out of the car.

She had changed into running gear before she left the apartment. Leggings, technical top, baseball cap pulled low. She had laced her shoes loosely on purpose.

She jogged at an easy pace along the opposite sidewalk, then crossed the street two houses up and came back along the driveway side.

The Audi was parked rear-out at the near end.

She stopped, crouched over her shoe, and took her time with the lace.

The tracker was under the rear bumper in four seconds. She stood, checked the knot, and jogged on.

At the end of the block she turned and didn't look back.
