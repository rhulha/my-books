The Nurse

Chapter 7 - The Nurse.

The door opened. A man in scrubs walked in. Young, maybe thirty, with a tablet under his arm and a badge that read "P. VASQUEZ - NURSING STAFF."

"Good morning, Mr. Murphy. My name is Peter. How are you feeling today?"

"Hi Peter, my name is Alex."

"Nice to meet you, Alex. I'm here to make sure you're comfortable. How is the angle of the bed?"

"It's fine."

"Great. Do you feel like you're getting enough rest?"

"Well, I sleep enough, yet I still feel a bit tired."

"Yeah, I heard that's normal in the beginning, but it's supposed to get better with time."

"Yeah, that's what they said."

He tapped something on his tablet. "Does the light bother you? I can adjust the blinds."

"Yeah, that would be great. A bit darker please."

Peter walked to the windows and tilted the blinds a little, then he came back to Alex and inspected him closely.

"Are you in any pain right now?"

"No."

Peter nodded and inspected Alex' metal body some more.

"Fascinating, if I may say so. I haven't seen anything like this before."

"Okay." He stepped back and looked at his tablet. "Your wife. Is she able to visit much?"

Alex looked at him. "Ellen comes every day."

"That's good. Family support is important." He scrolled. "And your daughter?"

Alex watched him.

"She has a bit of a hard time with this situation."

Peter nodded understandingly.

The nurse finished with the tube and set the tablet down on the windowsill. "Must be hard for her. Seeing you like this."

Alex started to feel a bit uncomfortable. "Yeah, it is."

"Kids are resilient." He picked up the tablet again. "How are you finding the new body? Is it easy to control?"

"Getting better."

"Does it feel like yours?"

"Look Peter, I mean no disrespect, but I'm really not in the mood to answer all of these personal questions right now."

A shot of red tinged Peter's cheeks.

"Oh, I'm sorry. Please accept my apologies. I just thought it would be good to get to know you a bit better. I understand if you don't want to talk about it. Again, so sorry!"

Alex looked at the ceiling. "It's okay. It's just all a bit much right now."

"Yes, of course," said Peter.

Alex heard a short knock and then Kam came in with a big smile.

His smile quickly faded when he saw the nurse.

"Hello," said Kam.

Peter just nodded at him and said "Well, I have to get going. I'll check in on you later, Alex."

He quickly turned towards the door and left the room.

Kam turned to Alex. "Who was that?"

"I have no idea, he said he's a nurse, but I haven't seen him before.

Alex hesitated for a moment, then said "And he asked me a lot of personal questions about my family and how I'm doing. It was a bit weird, to be honest."

Kam massaged his chin.

"Let me make a phone call."

And just as quickly as he came in, Kam left again.
