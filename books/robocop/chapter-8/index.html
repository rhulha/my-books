The OWL

Chapter 08: The OWL.

The brown cardboard box finally arrived.

She thanked the delivery person and took it inside.

It was bigger than she had expected, but that was probably just the packaging.

Sam set it on her kitchen table and cut the tape.

Yeah, packaging. Inside was the real box. A sleek black rectangle with a glossy finish. The label said "OWL - Aerial Surveillance Drone."

She lifted the inner box out and threw the outer one back at the door. The inner box was well crafted and pleasing to the eye. She set it down and carefully opened it.

The drone was nestled in a custom foam insert, with the controller and accessories neatly arranged around it.

The drone was smaller than she expected. Covered in digital camouflage, four rotors, a camera housing up front behind a wide lens. A grabbing arm folded flat against the undercarriage. A single latch released it.

She skimmed the quick start guide. The OWL was designed for surveillance and reconnaissance, with a focus on stealth and ease of use. It could be controlled via a smartphone app, which connected to the drone over a secure wireless link.

She read enough to feel comfortable. She downloaded the app and paired it with the drone.

It lifted off her kitchen table and hovered at eye level, almost silent. She walked a slow circle around it. The camera tracked her.

She tested the arm. On the app there was a grip icon. She tapped it and the arm extended and opened. Tapped again and it closed.

She held out her finger. The claw closed around it gently, like it was reading how much force to use.

"Nice," she said.

She flew it down the hallway, through the bathroom door, and back. It took the corners clean.

Then she put it back in the box and went to bed.
