Chapter 14.

Andy pushed his mashed potatoes around his plate while Debby served a small portion of salad to Gia.

"I hope you don't mind that I'm vegan," Gia said, her voice apologetic.

"Of course not." Debby smiled, setting down the salad bowl. "I admire it actually."

"Our generation messed up the planet and yours gonna have to save it," she continued with an apologetic smile.

Andy watched as Gia carefully speared a piece of lettuce with her fork. Her movements were precise, natural.
They had talked about the dinner privately and Gia had explained that she could store some food in the back of her throat, if it's not too much.
A little bit of food was no problem, but drinking was off the table. She would only pretend, they had decided.

"So, how was your first day?" Debby asked, cutting into her roast.

"The teachers were really nice," said Gia after storing a bit of salad. "Everyone was very welcoming. Mr. Anderson even spoke some Dutch."

"Oh, that's great!" Debby said, her eyes lighting up.

Gia nodded, pretending to take a small sip from her glass.

"I love Shakespeare, so it was nice to talk about his works in English," Gia continued.

Debby raised her eyebrows. "You like Shakespeare?"

Gia nodded. "I do. I've read a lot of his works in the original."

"We had to read Macbeth in school and I am sorry to say, well, I didn't really connect to it," Debby said, shaking her head.

"It's not for everyone," Gia said, taking another bite of salad.

"If that ain't the truth," Debby agreed with a smile.

"The school building is beautiful too, much bigger than what I'm used to."

Debby nodded. "I can imagine. Were the other students nice to you?"

"They were very friendly," Gia assured her, moving a piece of lettuce around her plate.

"But this Tom guy," Gia continued, "he's a real handful."

Debby laughed. "He sure is, but he has a good heart."

She glanced at Andy, who simply rolled his eyes before stuffing another piece of casserole into his mouth.
Gia and Andy had placed their glasses close together and in a moment of distraction, Andy quickly took Gia's.

"Speaking of students," Debby said, looking conspiratorially, "I heard an interesting rumor today."

Andy's fork froze halfway to his mouth.

"Mrs. Carter at the grocery store said her son Ryan saw you two holding hands in the hallway."

Andy forced a laugh. "Oh Mom, we were just playing a prank on Tom and some other students saw us. It was just a joke, okay?"

Debby nodded slowly. "I see."

"The whole school just went crazy because there's a new student. You know how rumors spread," continued Andy.

"It's true," Gia added. "Everyone was very excited about having an exchange student."

Debby put her fork and knife down, studying them both. "Well, in any case, the situation is as it is and I won't stand in the way of love."

Andy rolled his eyes again, "Mom!"

"Look, let's make this quick and painless for everyone," Debby said, leaning forward. 
"Should things get serious between the two of you, I want you to be safe."

"Oh my God!" said Andy, his cheeks burning red-hot. 
He pushed his plate away. He was done eating.

Gia nodded, completely unfazed by the topic. "Of course, Mrs. Thompson."
Debby forced a smile.

"See, we have at least two adults in the conversation. Would you like to join us?" Debby looked at Andy.

Andy shook his head, his face still burning.

"And please call me Debby." She raised her glass to Gia.
