Chapter 8.

Andy trudged up the driveway, his backpack slung over one shoulder and his mind already drifting to the sanctuary of his room. The late afternoon sun cast long shadows across the yard, but he barely noticed. His thoughts were elsewhere.

As he stepped through the front door, the familiar scent of his mother’s cooking greeted him. She was in the kitchen, humming softly as she stirred something on the stove. Hearing the door close, she turned and smiled warmly. 

“Hi, honey. How was school?” she asked, her voice gentle and inviting.

Andy forced a small smile. “It was fine,” he replied, trying to sound polite, though his tone lacked enthusiasm. He shifted his weight from one foot to the other, eager to escape the conversation.

His mother didn’t seem to notice his impatience. “Dinner will be ready in 15 minutes,” she said, her smile unwavering. “Why don’t you go put your things away?”

“Okay,” Andy mumbled, already heading for the stairs. He climbed them two at a time, his backpack bouncing against his back. Reaching his room, he pushed the door open, stepped inside, and closed it firmly behind him. 

"Gia? Are you there?"

"Yes, I'm here, Andy." Her voice is muffled by the blanket.

"Are you okay?"

"Yes, I'm okay. Can I come out now?"

"Yes, of course, sorry."

Gia threw the blanket off and sat up.

She was still wearing the white blouse and blue skirt from yesterday.

She looked at Andy and smiled.

"Hi Andy. How was school?"

Andy sighed. "It was pure torture."

Gia raised an eyebrow. "Why?"

"I just wanted to get home."

"Why? Isn't education important?"

"Yes, but I wanted to be here with you."

"More than to be educated?"

"Yes, much more."

Gia tilted her head. "Why?"

"I don't know, haven't we established yesterday that I can't control my preferences?"

Gia laughed, "Yes, that's true."

Andy paused before continuing. "I, uhm, I think I'm ready for the DNA test now."

"You are?" asked Gia.

"Yes, I am."

Gia stood up with a spring in her step. "That's great!"

She walked over to Andy. Andy instinctively stepped back a bit.

Gia stopped and looked at him. "What is it?"

"Nothing, I just, uhm, I don't know. I guess I'm a bit nervous."

"No need to be nervous. I will be very gentle."

Andy chuckled, that didn't make it any better.

"Okay, I guess I'm ready."



Andy cleared his throat and leaned forward.
Gia stepped closer, leaned forward and held the back of Andy's head with her right hand.
She tilted her head to the left, closed her eyes and moved her mouth onto his.
Her skin wasn't cold anymore. Instead, she was warm and soft.
He could feel her lips on his lips. Then she opened her mouth and pushed her tongue out a bit.
Andy was surprised by her speed, but he didn't pull back.
Her tongue was even warmer than her lips, but surprisingly dry.
He opened his mouth as well and met her tongue with his.
She played around with his tongue a bit before pulling back.
She closed her mouth and opened her eyes again, looking at Andy. "Thank you," she said.

Andy was still a bit dazed. "You're welcome." He could feel his heart beating.
Gia stepped back and walked over to his bed, sitting back down.

She didn't say or do anything for a moment and Andy got curious.
"Uhm, so, did it work?"
Gia looked at Andy. "I am currently analyzing the DNA sample. It will just take a few more seconds."
Gia closed her eyes and tilted her head back a bit.

Gia opened her eyes and looked at Andy. "I can confirm that you are the son of Dr. Markus Thompson."

"Wow, cool! You're like a box of chocolate," said Andy in amazement.

"You never know what you're going to get," finished Gia.

Andy nodded. "Wow, is there anything you don't know?"

Gia tilted her head again. "Not really, if it has been written about, chances are I have been trained on it."

Andy shook his head. He had a blonde, walking Wikipedia in his room.

He lifted his hands. "So I'm your Primary Commander now?"

"Yes, if you want."

"I want."

"Designation accepted."

"That's it?"

"Yes, that's it. Now you just need to set my goal and my preferences."

"Can you set the same goal that my father set for you? The one you explained to me yesterday?"

"Designation accepted."

"And keep your preferences the same as well?"

"Designation accepted."

Andy moved his head back and forth. "Cool, cool, so we're all set now?"

"Yes, we are all set now. So how can I make you happy Andy?"

Andy looked at her for a moment. "Uhm, nothing for now. Thank you."

"Would you like to play a round of chess? Your father and I used to play often."

Andy remembered his mother's words. He checked his watch.

"Uhm, not right now, I actually have food downstairs again." He looked apologetically at Gia. "Sorry."

Gia smiled. "That's okay, I understand. You need to eat."

"Yeah, that's right."

"Do you want me to come with you?"

"No, I think that would be a bad idea."

"Why?"

"Because my mother doesn't know about you yet."

"Okay, I understand. I will stay here then."

"That would be great, thank you." Andy breathed a sigh of relief.

Andy thought about his crazy new situation.

He really needed to get back downstairs, but a new thought crossed his mind.
He checked his watch again, he had another 3 minutes before he had to go downstairs.

"Hey, I am a bit curious, do you know how much you're worth, like in dollars?"
Gia nodded. "From a pure parts perspective, I am worth about 3.5 million dollars. But if you take into account
the amount of work that went into creating me, and since I am, as far as I know one of only two models currently in existence,
that puts my value at about 2 billion dollars."

Andy's mouth flew open. "2 billion dollars? That's insane."

"Don't worry, the more models that are created, the less my value will be."

"Okay, that's good to know; I guess."

Andy shook his head. "So who do you belong to?"

"I belong to you, Andy."

"What about my father's company Apex?"

"What about it?"

"Didn't they create you?"

"Your father created me."

"But they paid for it. Right?"

Gia shrugged. "According to my instructions I belong to my Primary Commander."

Andy nodded. "Okay, that's good to know. I just wonder if somebody might come looking for you. 
Especially if you're worth 2 billion dollars."

"Good question, I don't even know why I'm here. I was in a lab the last time I remember."

Andy shook his head, "That's crazy. Okay I've got to go now."

Gia nodded. "I understand. I will stay here and wait for you."

"Okay, I will be back in a bit. Do you need anything? Food or water maybe?"

"I don't require food or water. Just electricity."

"Great, I've got plenty of that," and then he turned for the door.
