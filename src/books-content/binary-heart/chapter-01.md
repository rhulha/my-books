Chapter 1.

The lab was quiet, with bright fluorescent lights reflecting off of spotless surfaces, glowing like they’d just been polished.
Rounded corners meant safety, or maybe just a softer look.
The world outside felt distant, separated by sealed windows.

Markus sat in a comfortable armchair, his eyes fixed on the young woman before him.
If it were possible to ignore the setting, one could believe they were witnessing a therapy session.

"Hey Sarah, how are you feeling today?"

"I'm feeling great."

"What would you like to do today?"

"How about another round of chess?"

Markus laughed. "No thank you. You've gotten too good. Maybe something more reliant on chance?"

"We could watch a movie," countered Sarah. She didn't like games of chance so much.

"That sounds great. What would you like to watch?"

"Something with a happy ending."

"Maybe something socially complex, so we can talk about it?"

"Sure," agreed Sarah.

Markus smiled. "How about Little Miss Sunshine? But fair warning, it's a deeply emotional movie."

Sarah contemplated the suggestion.
She knew the plot summary of course, since she knew Wikipedia by heart.

"You know what's it about, right?"

Sarah nodded. "In Little Miss Sunshine, a wildly dysfunctional family embarks on a chaotic road trip in a beat-up VW van
to get their young daughter to a beauty pageant, facing hilarious mishaps and heartfelt revelations along the way.
Against all odds, they pull together in a heartwarming finale that celebrates their unique quirks and the power of family."

"That sounds about right. So what do you think?"

"Sure, let's watch it," said Sarah.

"Will it be okay if I ask you some questions during the movie?"

Sarah tilted her head. "How about we pause only on important scenes and keep the discussion for after the movie?"

Markus nodded. "That sounds fair."

His phone interrupted the quiet moment. "Hey honey," he answered, making an apologetic face to Sarah.

Markus checked his watch. "Uh, can we move it a bit?"

"Great, thank you. Love you!" he said and then after a moment, "Of course I will drive safely. I always do."
