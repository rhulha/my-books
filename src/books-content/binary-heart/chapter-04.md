Chapter 4.

They had finally gotten everything that would fit stored in boxes, and Debby announced she would drive them to the library immediately.
Andy offered to help, but she declined, asking him to prepare dinner and a good movie instead.

Debby pointed at the oven timer.

"I have put a roast in the oven with a timer, so that part should be automated.
But just to be sure, please keep it company and check in on it regularly," she said with a smile. "I don't want it to burn just in case."

"Got it. What should we have with the roast?" Andy asked.

"What do you want with it?" Debby replied.

Andy thought for a moment. "How about mashed potatoes and green beans?"

Debby checked the freezer and pulled out a pack of green beans.
Then she went for the pantry and pulled out a bag of mashed potato mix.
She handed them to Andy. "Sounds good. Can you handle that?"
"Mom, of course," Andy replied, slightly offended.

Debby nodded, picking up the car keys. "I'll be back in an hour, tops."

"Who will help you with the boxes at the library?" Andy asked with real concern.

"I phoned the university. They have a couple of students ready to help me unload. They get this sort of thing a couple of times per year they told me."

"Cool," Andy nodded, impressed.

"See you later!" she called from the door. "Don't forget the roast! If it's burned, you'll be in trouble!"

"No worries!" Andy called back, then the door closed with a soft thud behind his mother.

Andy thought about a plan. He would first prepare the mashed potatoes and the beans and then put them on the stove on super low heat to keep it warm.
He placed the beans onto a plate and placed it in the microwave. After setting the timer, he pressed start.
"Okay," he said to himself, "that's done."
He opened the refrigerator and took out the milk and some butter.
The mashed potatoes were quickly prepared and put on the stove on very low heat.
The beans were done by now, but he kept them in the microwave.
"They should be fine," he said to himself.
Finally, he quickly checked in on the roast.
It was still baking, but the smell was already amazing.
Then he darted back to his room.
