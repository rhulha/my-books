Chapter 16.

Tom and Andy sat on the bleachers during PE, watching their athletic classmates play basketball, both of them benched for the current rotation.
The squeak of sneakers and shouts echoed through the gym.

"Dude, you have to tell me your secret," Tom said.
"What secret?" Andy kept his eyes on the game, feigning ignorance.
"Andy, come on, you know exactly what I'm talking about."
Andy sighed, "Look, she's an exchange student from Europe and I got lucky that she picked my house. I look cool to her for the next few days until she realizes how hot she is and then she will go out with someone else."
"You fucking liar," Tom said. "She doesn't take her eyes off of you."
Andy shrugged. "I don't know what to tell you."
Tom leaned in closer. "Dude I've known you since kindergarten. I can feel that you're hiding something from me."
Andy looked at Tom. It was burning him up inside not to tell him. He wanted to tell him so badly.
"Come on Andy, I know there is something that you want to tell me, but don't. Did you save her life? Are you secretly rich?"
Andy sighed, watching Ryan hit a three pointer, of course.
"Dude, I'm your best friend. Why can't you tell me?"
Andy looked at Tom, who was watching him expectantly.

The truth pressed against Andy's chest like a xenomorph. He wanted to share the truth.
It physically pained him every second he kept it to himself.

"Okay Tom, if I tell you the real reason, you have to swear not to tell it to anyone ever."
Andy looked at Tom with the stare of death himself.

Tom even got scared for a moment. He had never seen Andy so serious before.
He raised one finger. "Pinky swear." He didn't smile.

"I am deadly serious, Tom. You have no idea how serious this is to me."

"I promise, Andy. I swear on my life." The curiosity was killing Tom.

"Say after me: I, Tom Gardner, swear on my life that I will keep secret what my friend Andy is about to tell me about Gia."

Tom looked around, no one cared for their existence.

"I, Tom Gardner, swear on my life that I will keep secret what my friend Andy is about to tell me about Gia."

"Say it again."

"Are you fucking serious, Andy?"

Andy nodded.

Tom sighed. "I, Tom Gardner, swear on my life that I will keep secret what my friend Andy is about to tell me about Gia."

"You will keep it fucking secret, Tom. You will never tell anyone. Not a soul. Not your own mother."

Tom nodded, "Jesus fucking Christ Andy, did you two murder someone?"

"Say it."

"I will take it to the fucking grave Andy. Now fucking tell me!" Tom started to get seriously pissed.

Andy took a deep breath. He looked around; no one cared. No one was close.
Then he whispered, "Gia is an experimental humanoid robot from my father's company Apex Automation."

Tom's mouth opened; he forgot to breathe for a moment, then he caught himself.

"Bullshit."

Andy relaxed back into his seat, enjoying the moment.

"No way."

Andy pretended to suddenly care for the game again.

"Andy." Andy didn't react. "Andy, look at me."

Andy chuckled. "Dude! Look at me."

Andy looked at Tom with fake indignation.

"Say that again."

Andy lowered his voice again. "Gia is an experimental humanoid robot from my father's company Apex Automation."

"What the fuck?!" exclaimed Tom.

Andy leaned back again, watching the game.

"Can I have one too?" asked Tom, shaking Andy's shoulder.

"No, it's pretty clear that Apex doesn't know I have her. My father brought her home in secret it seems. She is worth 2 billion and if they find out, they will obviously take her from me. And that's why you have to keep your fucking mouth shut."

Tom looked like Martians were invading Earth.

"2 billion?"

"Yeah, but only because of development cost, the pure parts are apparently 2.5 million."

"So you think they are building girls so people can buy them?"

Andy shrugged, "Looks like it?"

"Is she, you know, anatomically correct?"

"Tom, I can't believe that's the first question you have about all of this."

Tom chuckled. "Technically it's my second." Tom thought about the conversation so far. "Or third even."

Andy sighed.

"So is she?"

"Is she what."

Tom checked his shoulder against Andy's. "Dude, come on. You know exactly what I'm talking about."

"No she is not, she looks pretty much like Barbie down there."

"Haha, I knew it. I knew you checked."

Andy rolled his eyes. "Yes I did, you happy now?"

Tom chuckled. "So what do you do with her?"

Andy shrugged. "I spend time with her. She's really great. She knows a lot, like a walking encyclopedia. She's really good at chess, she's a great cook, she's a great listener. She likes to watch movies too."

"Wow, sounds like the perfect girlfriend." Tom was starting to get deeply jealous. "So does she kiss?"

"Yes, but it's very dry."

"Dry? What do you mean?"

"She doesn't have saliva."

"Wow, that's insane." Tom was deep in thought now. "So you think I can buy one soon too?"

"My guess is, they will be crazy expensive in the beginning."

Tom nodded, his hopes being crushed. Then he had a new thought.

"Share her!"

"What?"

"Share her with me."

"No, Tom, I will not share my girlfriend with you."

"Andy, you just told me she isn't real. We used to share everything non-human in the past?"

Andy stopped pretending to focus on the game. "Tom, this is a crazy situation for me too, do you understand?"

Tom nodded.

"Yes, she's not human, but she's not a skateboard either. And the truth is I have some sort of feelings for her. Can you please give me space to figure this all out?"

Tom nodded quickly. "Yeah, sure."

"Okay, thank you. You wanted me to tell you my secret. I told you. I have so many things to think about at the moment, I really need your help to figure this all out to be honest."

Andy looked at Tom with a serious face.

"Okay, Andy, I understand. I'm sorry. Holy shit man, this is so exciting. I don't know what to say."

"Look, we can meet at my place later today and I can show her to you, like her real side."

"That would be awesome."

"Okay, deal. Come over after school, I can ask mom if you can join us for dinner."

Tom nodded eagerly.

"Okay, remember to shut up about this. No insider jokes, no quips, no funny questions."

Tom motioned to lock his lips and throw away the key.

"She is an exchange student from the Netherlands. Got it?"

"Got it!" responded Tom.

They sat together in silence, then Tom had to chuckle.

"What?" asked Andy.

"It's kind of funny."

"What is?"

"Thinking about what is more likely to be true, you having a hot girlfriend or you finding a 2 billion dollar android."

Andy wanted to protest for a moment, but then he had to laugh too. "Yeah, that is kinda funny."
