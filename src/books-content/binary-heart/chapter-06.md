Chapter 6.

Her eyelids suddenly fluttered open, revealing deep blue eyes.
"Whoa!" Andy gasped and stepped away from his bed, almost tripping over his own feet.

The girl's eyes darted around the room.
Then her arms began to move, pushing down and back, lifting her upper body.
She turned her head and looked at Andy.

"Hello, my name is Gia." Her voice was steady yet disarmingly soft.

Andy was speechless, still taking little steps backwards.

"¿Hablas español?" asked Gia in perfect Spanish. Andy shook his head.
"Do you speak English?"
Andy nodded.
"Hello, my name is Gia. May I know your name?"

Andy cleared his throat. "Andy. My name is Andy."

"Hello Andy. Do you know where Dr. Markus Thompson is?"

"Uh… hi, Gia. He… well, he is no longer with us," Andy said, trying to sound as calm as possible.

"What do you mean?"

"He... well, he passed away. Unfortunately," said Andy.

Gia blinked. "Oh, I'm sorry to hear that. Dr. Markus Thompson was my Primary Commander."

"Your Primary Commander?" Andy asked.

"Yes, are you related to Dr. Markus Thompson?"

Andy nodded. "Well, actually yes." Andy paused for a moment, before continuing. "I'm his son."

"May I verify that?"

"Verify that?" Andy looked puzzled. "What, that I'm the son of Markus Thompson?"

"Yes." Gia blinked her eyes.

Andy shrugged his shoulders. "Okay, sure. You wanna see my ID?"

Gia pushed the blanket off of her and swung her legs down the side of the bed.

"Careful." Andy pointed at the power cable.

But Gia just looked at the cable and nodded, before standing up and walking over to Andy.
The cable was long enough and Andy moved back, bumping into his desk. 
This was not at all what he had signed up for today.

Gia reached out and touched Andy's face. "May I kiss you?"
Andy crashed into his desk, dangerously shaking his computer monitor.
"Whoa! Wait a minute, no you may not kiss me."

Gia took a step back, visibly concerned about Andy's reaction.
"I apologize. I'm not trying to be improper. I'm just trying to verify your statement."

Andy took a deep breath. His desk could settle a bit.
"Wait, please, give me a minute. A second ago you were lying in my bed, like a corpse. And now you want to kiss me. This is a lot to process."

Gia nodded. "I understand. I am sorry for the inconvenience."
Andy pushed his chair a bit so it moved until he could sit down.

"This is crazy!" he said. "Do you understand how crazy this is?"
Gia shook her head. "Not really. Is this the first time that you're talking to an android?"

Andy raised his eyebrows. "An android?"

Gia nodded. "Yes, I'm an advanced humanoid robot, also known as an android. Series GX-7."

Andy took a deep breath. He knew his father had been working on artificial intelligence, but Andy could have never imagined anything like what he was seeing in his own room now.

"Is there any way I can make you more comfortable?" asked Gia.

Andy thought for a moment. "Well, yes. You could start by explaining why you wanted to kiss me."

Gia nodded. "Sure. I am trying to verify your statement that you are the son of Dr. Markus Thompson.
I can do that by comparing your DNA to his. I can get a sample of your DNA by kissing you."

Andy blinked. "Oh, I see." Andy felt relieved. "I thought you were trying to assimilate me or something."
Gia looked surprised. "Assimilate you? What do you mean by that?"

Andy chuckled and shook his head. "Never mind. I think I've been watching too much Star Trek."

Gia looked at Andy expectantly. "So, may I kiss you now?"

"Woah, woah, woah," Andy said. "Easy there young lady. I mean, I don't know you. You could be a serial killer or something."

Gia smiled. "You're very funny Andy. Just like your father."

Andy smiled back. "Thanks, I guess. So you do believe me then?"

Gia tilted her head a bit, "Well, I will grant you the benefit of the doubt for the sake of the conversation, but in order to take you as my Primary Commander I really do need proof."

"What happens if you don't get my DNA sample?" asked Andy.

"I can't take you as my Primary Commander."

"Okay, so you need to kiss me to verify that I'm the son of Dr. Markus Thompson?"

Gia nodded. "Yes, that is correct."

"That seems a bit odd, no? Did my father really program you to require a kiss for verification?"

"No, not necessarily a kiss, but a DNA sample. You can also swab the inside of your mouth with a q-tip if you want, but it would just add a layer of indirection."

"So the sensor for checking DNA is in your mouth?" Andy asked, still trying to wrap his head around this.

"Yes, that is correct. I believe there is a plan for a future version of me to have a sensor in my hand, but for now it's in my mouth."

Andy looked uneasy.

Gia continued. "I can see that you are uncomfortable with this. Look, this is not a planned procedure, there was no plan to ever have to switch my Primary Commander since I am a test model. It just happens to be the case, that I can switch my Primary Commander if I get a DNA sample showing a direct relation to my last Primary Commander and that my DNA sensor is in my mouth."

Andy thought about it for a moment.
"Okay, so if I let you kiss me, then you can verify that I'm the son of Dr. Markus Thompson and then I can be your Primary Commander?"

Gia nodded again. "Yes, that is correct."

Andy shook his head. What a crazy situation he found himself in.
He didn't want to stop talking to Gia, but he also needed to get back downstairs and check on the food.
The oven was off, the peas were waiting, but he really should check in on the mashed potatoes.

"And what does it mean to be your Primary Commander?" asked Andy instead.

Gia smiled. "That's a great question. As my Primary Commander, you will be responsible for setting my 
goals and preferences."

"Your goals and preferences?" Andy asked, confused.

Gia nodded eagerly. "Yes."

"Why do you need someone to set your goals and preferences?" asked Andy, still confused.

"I'm an advanced humanoid robot. Series GX-7. I'm trained and capable of making decisions on my own.
However, I require a Primary Commander to make decisions that are not within my programming."

"Like what?"

"Like my goals and preferences."

"So you don't have any goals or preferences at the moment?"

"I do, I keep my goals and preferences from my last Primary Commander until I get new ones," explained Gia.

Andy was puzzled. This was all a bit much. He shook his head.

"I may sound stupid, but what's the difference between goals and preferences, in like, your context?"

"That's not a stupid question at all and I'd be happy to explain." Gia smiled before continuing.

"Goals are specific objectives that a person actively works toward achieving. They usually require effort, planning, and commitment."

Andy nodded. "Okay, I get that."

"Preferences, on the other hand, are more like inclinations or tendencies toward certain options. They are not necessarily tied to specific actions or outcomes."

Andy nodded again. "Okay, I think I get it now. Goals are the things I want to achieve and preferences are the things I like or dislike."

"Exactly," said Gia. "For example, I prefer to eat pizza over broccoli, but my goal is to eat a balanced diet."

"You like pizza?" asked Andy, surprised.

"No, as I said, that was just an example. In order to not fill up my small food storage compartment too fast I pretend to prefer vegetables like broccoli."

"Your food storage compartment?" Andy asked, this was getting stranger and stranger.

"Yes, I have a small food storage compartment in my throat so I can pretend to eat food."

"So you can better appear human?"

"Yes, exactly. I can store food, but I don't digest it. I just do it to fit in."

Andy shook his head. "Okay, so about those preferences. You want me to set them for you?"

Gia nodded twice in quick succession.

"It's kinda funny to think about this," Andy said. "I mean, I don't even know where my own preferences came from."

"Oh that's totally normal for humans. Some preferences are learned from experience, while others are innate or instinctual, but nothing is decided."

"Hold on a minute, are you saying that I don't control my own preferences?"

"Yes, exactly. Think about any preference that you have."

Andy looked at the ceiling for a moment then he looked back at Gia.

"Okay, easy, I have a preference for pizza over broccoli."

Gia smiled. "Great. Now, when did you decide that?"

Andy stared at Gia. He tried to remember when he had decided that he liked pizza more than broccoli.

He couldn't remember. He tried harder. Then he shrugged.

"That's crazy. Okay, let's try with something else, something serious this time."

He seriously needed to check on the food downstairs, he thought, but he didn't move an inch.

Gia smiled, "Do you listen to girls or boys?"

Blood shot into Andy's cheeks. "Girls," he said with a shy smile.

"Okay, when did you decide that?"

He thought about it for a moment and then he realized that Gia was absolutely correct.

"That's crazy, I never thought about it like this before. You're right, I don't decide my preferences. I just have them."

"Exactly," Gia said. "You have them, but you don't decide them. You can change some of them some over long periods of time, but it usually requires a lot of effort and self-reflection."

Andy nodded. He really needed to go downstairs. Now.

"Hey Gia, I have food cooking downstairs. I really need to check on it."

"Oh great, can I help?" Gia asked excitedly.

"Uh, no. It's all ready, just waiting on the stove in low heat mode."

"Okay, I'll wait here then." Gia said, smiling.

"Thank you," Andy said. He turned around and walked to the door. "Be right back."

He rushed down the stairs and into the kitchen.

The smell of the roast was filling the kitchen and Andy could see the mashed potatoes bubbling in the pot.
He quickly checked the peas and they were still in the microwave, waiting for him to take them out.
He took a wooden spoon and stirred the mashed potatoes. All good.
Finally he rushed back to the stairs and up to his room.

For a moment he had doubts, that there really would be a young woman waiting.

But there she was again.

"I'm back," he said, closing the door behind him.

"Welcome back Andy," Gia said, smiling. "Is the food alright?"

"Yes, everything is fine. What were we talking about?"

Gia smiled. "We were talking about preferences and how they are not something you can just change at will."

"Right, right. For a human at least, but you can get them set by your Primary Commander."

"Yes, that is correct."

Andy nodded; he kinda started to understand.

Andy leaned back in his chair. "This is so weird. In a way you're asking me to program your personality."
"Yes," Gia said. "And that is exactly the point of it."

"What goal did my father give you?"

"He told me my goal is to maximize the happiness of the people around me based on care. The amount of care I feel for someone should be proportional to the time I've spent with that person, with my Primary Commander being the person I care about the most."

Andy thought for a moment. "Uhm, that's a pretty cool goal actually. Could you just keep it?"

"I already have it set. Remember I keep my goals until my Primary Commander gives me new ones."

Andy nodded slowly.

"So you don't really need a Primary Commander then?"

Gia tilted her head. "Well, in case I get conflicting orders, I will run into deadlock. So it is best that I have a Primary Commander."

"Yeah, that makes sense." Andy said. "Can I think about it for a night?"

"Of course, take your time."

Andy thought about that for a moment before a new smell started to hit his nose. A burnt smell.

"Oh fuck, my mother will kill me!" he shouted, before darting out of his room.
