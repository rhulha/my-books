Chapter 5.

Andy opened the door to his room. For a moment he doubted that he had a lifeless woman in his bed.
But there it was, an unusual bump in his bed. He left the door open so he could hear if his mother returned early.

Approaching the bed slowly, Andy's heart started to beat faster.
He reached out and clasped the top edge of the blanket, pulling it back slowly.
First, some hair was revealed, then a forehead, and finally a face.
Gasping, he realized it looked like a real human, yet it couldn't be.
Pulling the blanket further down, he uncovered her upper body.
She was wearing a white blouse with blue stripes.

Dropping the blanket, he reached out with trembling fingers.
The side of her face felt cold under his touch, like silicone, but underneath was something solid.
Up close, her skin had an artificial sheen, but you really had to look for it.

Why did his father have a doll of a young woman in his closet?
Did his father have a different side, a side that Andy didn't know about?
Did his mother know?

"What the hell, Dad?" he muttered, shaking his head in disbelief.
His father, a man of logic and reason, had a secret more bewildering than anything he could've ever imagined.
Swallowing hard, his mind tried to reconcile the uncanny scene.

Only one way to find out.
He pulled the blanket all the way down and uncovered a blue skirt as well as her legs.
His heart was racing. Never before had he seen anything like this.
But he had to know the truth. With careful hands, he reached out and touched the seam of her skirt.
Pulling it up carefully, he saw that she was wearing white underwear.
Andy placed the seam of the skirt on her belly and took a deep breath.
Here goes nothing, he thought.
He pulled the white underwear to the side and saw... Skin.
Nothing else, just skin. Like a Barbie doll.

Andy let go of the underwear and it snapped back into place.
He pulled the skirt back down and covered her legs with the blanket.

Then he stood up and started walking in circles.
"This just doesn't make any sense," he said out loud. "Dad, what the hell?!"

He got a bit dizzy, so he decided to sit down at his desk.
Andy looked at the black screen of his computer monitor. "What's the fucking point?"
He shook his head before spinning around in his chair, looking at the face of the young woman in his bed, whenever he came to face her.

On the third turn he stopped and paused for a moment. A new idea formed.

Andy stood up, walked over to the bed and sat down on the edge.
He started to investigate her body again, but this time the other parts.
He touched her nose, touched her cheeks, opened her mouth and looked inside. He touched her throat.
Everything felt and looked kind of correct, only way too cold.
Is this what it feels like being a coroner? he wondered.

Her skin smelled a bit like silicone, but not too much.
He touched her neck and her shoulders.

After a moment's hesitation, he looked back at the door. Nobody was there.
Then he looked back at the young woman in his bed and touched her breasts, only for science, of course.
Well, maybe he touched a bit long for science. He moved on.

Following the lines of her belly and her hips with his hands, he suddenly felt something on the right side of her hip from his perspective.
Leaning over her body, he inspected the anomaly.
He pulled the skirt up and the underwear down a bit.
There was something hard, metallic, a small socket.
"Holy cow," he exclaimed.

Throwing the blanket back over her, he rushed for the door.
He desperately wanted to check the closet for a power cable, but reason prevailed and he decided to check on the kitchen first.

Running down the stairs in double time, he crossed the living room and entered the kitchen to check on the food.
The pot with mashed potatoes got a quick stir—all good.
The beans in the microwave were fine too.
A glance at the roast in the oven showed it was looking good, though maybe a bit dark.
He turned off the heat and closed the oven.

Then, he turned, crossed the living room again and ran up the stairs, heading for the office.
Darting into his father's old room, he paced towards the open closet.
He rummaged through the boxes and quickly found what he was looking for: A modern looking power converter and a plug.

It looked exactly like the kind of plug that would fit into the socket of the woman in his bed.
Grabbing the converter, he ran back into his room.
Near his bed, a power outlet caught his eye.
He plugged the power converter into the outlet and pulled the cable and plug over to the young woman.
With careful precision, he pushed the plug into the socket on her hip.
Looking at her face, he waited eagerly for something to happen. But nothing did.
Minutes passed as he sat on his bed just looking at her.
Long blonde hair framed her face, nice clothes complemented her slim figure. A sleeping beauty.

He imagined charging a mobile phone that had been empty for a long time.
That usually took a little while before showing any sign of life too.
A glance at the power converter showed a small light on it.
Just now it started to blink.
