Chapter 4: Enough Is Enough.

Sam had been sitting in the car for twenty minutes.

She wasn't sure what she had expected. Something harder to see, maybe.

But there they were. Three of them, at the far end of the park near the basketball court. One watched the street. One watched the path. The third one handled the transactions.

She turned the ring on her left hand. The stone was dark blue, small, and cut in a simple round. Most people assumed it was sapphire. She never corrected them.

One of the dealers laughed at something. The sound carried.

She pulled out her phone. The screen showed a product page: the Owl, a small, sleek surveillance drone.

Forty-minute battery on active flight. Eighty hours on perch mode.

She tapped "Buy now."

She set her phone down and looked at the park.

The dealer on the corner made another exchange and pocketed the cash without breaking stride.

Sam started the car.
