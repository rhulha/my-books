Sam - Chapter 16: The House.

The tracker had pinged from the same address three nights in a row.

Sam looked it up on the county assessor's site. A name came back that meant nothing to her. She cross-referenced it against the two faces from the park. No match. Probably a relative, or nobody.

She drove out on a Sunday afternoon.

The neighborhood was on the east edge of the city where the streets widened and the lots got bigger. Not wealthy exactly, but comfortable. Lawns that got mowed. Driveways that got sealed.

She slowed as she passed the house. Two-story brick, detached garage on the left, a concrete driveway wide enough for three cars. A black pickup and a white sedan were parked outside. The Chrysler wasn't there.

She kept driving, turned at the end of the block, and came back around.

On the second pass she spotted a utility pole.

It was on the far side of the street, set back slightly, with a clear line of sight to the front of the house and most of the driveway. A cross-arm near the top had a horizontal beam wide enough.

She found a spot three houses down and parked facing away from the house.

She opened the app and launched the OWL from the back seat.

She flew it low along the row of parked cars, keeping it reasonably high, then brought it down once it was level with the pole. The camera showed the cross-arm close up, rough wood, a bolt sticking out at the end.

She extended the arm and moved in slowly.

The claw found the bolt and closed.

The rotors spun down.

She panned the camera left until the house filled the frame. Front door. Garage door. The driveway. All of it clear.

She tapped record.

Then she put the car in drive and headed back toward the city.
