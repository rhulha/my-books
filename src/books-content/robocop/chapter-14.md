Sam - Chapter 14: The Tracker.

She was back at the park by seven in the evening.

The thugs usually came around 7:30, but she wanted to make sure.

Opposite side street.

She had brought a thermos of Earl Grey and listened to The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy.

7:35 still no sign of them. She started to get nervous.

Had they spotted her? Had they changed their plans?

She shook her head, stop worrying. They were probably just late.

She took a sip of her tea and relaxed a little.

*The ships hung in the sky in much the same way that bricks don't.*

At 7:41 a dark blue Chrysler rolled slowly past the park entrance and turned into the small lot on the south side. It sat there for a moment before the doors opened.

Two men got out and looked around. One was wearing a gray jacket and the other a red cap.

They headed into the park and disappeared from view.

Sam's heart started to beat faster. She scrambled, putting the tea aside and grabbing the tracker from the passenger seat.

Magnetic mount, civilian grade, but she had reflashed the firmware herself. It would ping once per hour instead of every thirty seconds. Harder to sweep for.

She walked at a normal pace, the tracker in her fist.

The Chrysler was second from the end. She crouched as she passed the rear bumper, reached under the chassis, and pressed the tracker against the frame. The magnet caught with a quiet click.

She kept walking, rounded the block, and came back to her car from the other direction.

She started the engine and pulled out before the two men were back in view.

On her phone the tracker was already showing a position. Parked. Stationary.

She merged onto the main road and headed home.
