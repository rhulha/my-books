Chapter 13 - A New Start.

Alex opened his eyes.

The bariatric chair was in the corner of the living room.

Reinforced steel frame, a wide leather pad, huge armrests. OCP had delivered it the day before he came home.

He looked at his hands. He flexed the fingers. The servos whirred. He removed the charging cable from his leg and stood up.

The room was the same. The same photo on the mantel. The same lamp Ellen had picked out two years ago. The same stain on the rug from when Jamie was eight.

He looked around, it felt nice to see familiar surroundings.

He looked at his hands, they were robotic now. He moved his legs.

He still couldn't believe it all.

How had this happened to him?

He decided to make breakfast. Doing something normal.

He stood up, feeling a bit shaky.

He gained balance and started to walk into the kitchen. The first few steps were awkward, but he quickly adapted. Kam had trained him well.

The sound of his servos filled the quiet morning air.

He opened the fridge, taking in the familiar sight of his favorite breakfast items. He would never eat them again.

He tried to grab a carton of orange juice, that worked well.

He placed it on the kitchen counter.

Next he grabbed the bacon. The package was small and he struggled to get a good grip. After a few attempts, he managed to grab a hold on it and he placed it on the kitchen counter.

Next, he tried to grab the eggs. The carton was larger and the risk even greater.

He knew, that if he dropped it, it would be a mess.

He carefully tried to grab the carton, trying again and again until he had a secure grip.

After a few attempts, he managed to hold it steady and placed it on the counter next to the bacon and orange juice.

He grabbed a frying pan and placed it on the stove. Not perfectly, but good enough. He turned on the burner and waited for it to heat up.

He tried to open the bacon. He didn't have a chance the normal way.

So he just ripped it open. The sound of tearing plastic filled the kitchen.

He placed strips of bacon in the pan. The sizzling sound was loud and satisfying.

Now he tried to crack the eggs.

He managed to open the carton, but taking out an egg was a different story.

He took one, but it immediately cracked. Still no problem, he moved it over the pan and used his other hand to open the shell and pour the contents into the pan. The yolk broke and spread out, but it cooked just fine.

Alex felt a tinge of relief.

One down, five more to go.

He continued to crack the rest of the eggs, each one breaking in a similar way, but they all ended up in the pan.

Unfortunately, the process was slow and messy and the bacon and the first egg were starting to burn. He quickly turned down the heat and tried to salvage what he could.

But then the fire alarm went off, filling the kitchen with a loud, piercing sound.

Alex cursed, thinking about crushing the alarm with his fists.

"Alex, what are you doing?" Ellen's voice came from the doorway as she rushed into the kitchen.

She looked at the mess on the stove and the broken eggshells on the counter.

She took the pan, laid a cork trivet in the middle of the table and placed the pan on top of it. Then she opened a window.

"I'm sorry," said Alex.

"Don't worry about it," said Ellen. "Looks like I came just in time." She smiled.

She grabbed the spatula from Alex's black hands and managed to salvage most of the bacon and eggs.

"But maybe, next time, we can try something a little simpler for breakfast?" she suggested with a grin.

Alex nodded. He could punch a hole in the wall right about now, but he knew Ellen was right. He needed to start with something simpler and work his way up.

"Oooh, what happened here?" Jamie's voice came from the doorway as she walked into the kitchen.

"Nothing, just eggs well done," said Ellen.

"I tried to make breakfast," said Alex.

Jamie looked at him, it was still incredible to see him like this.

"I bet it tastes great," she said with a slight stutter and a forced smile.

She grabbed the orange juice and three cups.

Then she slowly placed one cup back, glancing at Alex, checking if he had seen her taking three.

He had and blood started to rush into her face.

She quickly put the two cups on the table and tried to act normal.

She sat down and poured herself and her mom a cup of orange juice and took a sip.

Ellen placed two plates on the table and evenly distributed bacon and eggs.

Alex was unsure if he could sit on the thin wood chairs.

They would have to get new ones, but for now, he kept standing.

Another step on the awkwardness ladder, Alex sighed.

Jamie took a bite of the bacon and egg.

"Hmm, this is really good," said Jamie.

Ellen took a bite as well. "Very good," she agreed.

"Liars!" Alex said.

This finally broke the tension and they all laughed.
