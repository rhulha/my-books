Sam - Chapter 12: Bingo.

She plugged the drone into her laptop and waited for the transfer.

72 hours and forty minutes of footage.

She opened her video analysis tool and dropped the file in. She typed the prompt into the field:

*Focus on the drug sellers. Ignore all drug sales from sellers to individual buyers. Flag all persons approaching sellers that take money or give more drugs.*

She hit analyse and went to make some earl grey.

She added a splash of milk and a teaspoon of sugar.

She took a sip and went back to the screen.

She looked at the timeline. The tool had flagged 2 interactions so far.

She clicked the first one.

Dogs. Joggers. A man who stopped to argue with one of the dealers and walked away without buying anything. A kid on a bike.

Then, at timestamp 4:22:07:

Two guys approached the first seller from the south path. One heavyset in a gray jacket, one lean in a red cap. They moved like they'd done it a hundred times. No eye contact with anyone else, no hesitation. One transferred a large quantity of packaged goods to the seller, who transferred cash back. Duration: 38 seconds.

Sam scrubbed back and tried to find a clear view.

She found one and took a screenshot.

Then she clicked the second flagged interaction.

Same guys, same interaction, even better view.

She scrubbed frame by frame until she had a clean shot of each face.

She took another screenshot and hit print.
