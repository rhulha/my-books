Chapter 3 - Waking up.

They walked barefoot along the shore in San Vito Lo Capo.
The air and sand was still warm.
The moon and a couple of beach lanterns painted shadows of their legs.
He drew Ellen closer.

"Mr. Murphy, can you hear me?"

"Mr. Murphy, can you hear me? Ah now."

"Don't worry Mr. Murphy, you don't need to say anything. I can see if you hear me on my monitor."

"My name is Samantha and I will help you move again, okay? I'm here with Dr. Ellison."

"Hello Alex, everything is alright, just try to relax and follow Sam's instructions," said Dr. Ellison.

Then he nodded at Sam to continue.

"Let's start with your eye lids. Can you try to open them for me? Don't worry if it doesn't work right away. It will in a moment."

"Great, I have a reading. Give me a... moment... got it! Please try again."

Alex tried to open his eyes again. This time it worked.

A flush of light filled his vision. He blinked a couple of times to adjust.

He saw what appeared to be a white ceiling.

"Good, you're doing great Alex," said Sam. "Now, can you try to move your eyes a bit?"

"Again, don't worry if it doesn't work right away."

Alex tried to move his eyes, but it didn't work. Why couldn't he move his eyes? What the hell was going on?

He tried to move his arms and body, anything, but nothing happened.

"Please relax Mr. Murphy, you're doing great," said Sam soothingly.

"Everything will work very soon, I promise, we just have to take things step by step, okay?"

"Please try to move your eyes again."

Suddenly Alex could move his eyes again. Finally, some sort of control.

He looked around, trying to make sense of his surroundings.

He saw the head of a woman but not much more than the hairline.

Next to her stood a man in a white coat. Probably the doctor.

He looked concerned or was it curiosity?

"Excellent, Alex, please move your eyes up and down two times."

Alex focused and moved his eyes up and down twice.

"Great job, Alex! Next we will work on your voice. Try to say something."

He tried to open his mouth and say Hi, but it didn't work.

"Don't worry, Alex. Just keep trying," said Sam encouragingly. "It will work in a second."

"H... e... l... l... o..." croaked Alex, his voice sounded weird.

"Awesome! It will get better with time," said Sam. "The system is learning your brain patterns."

My brain patterns? Alex thought. What would a system need to learn his brain patterns for him to speak?

Dr. Ellison stepped closer. "You're doing great Alex. We are almost done with the first phase. Please try and follow my finger with your eyes."

He held up his finger and moved it side to side.

Alex focused on the finger and followed it with his eyes.

"Perfect! How many fingers am I holding up?" asked Dr. Ellison.

"One," croaked Alex.

"That is correct!" said Dr. Ellison before he turned to Sam. "He's responding well."

She nodded.

Sam took over again. "Alright Alex, let's try something else now. Please move your right arm. And as always, don't worry if it doesn't work right away."

Alex tried to move his right arm, but nothing happened.

What on earth were they doing, that everything only ever worked on the second try?

"Okay, got the reading, try again Alex."

Alex tried again and as before, this time it worked. He could feel it moving.

But every time it moved, he heard a weird mechanical sound.

He tried angling his arm, moving his fingers. Every move was met with the same mechanical sound.

"What... is... that... sound?" asked Alex.

Sam looked at Dr. Ellison, who turned to Alex again. "Don't worry about the sound for now, Alex, just focus on the instructions, okay?"

Alex had a weird feeling in his gut. "Where is my Family?"

"Your family is here, they will come in to see you in a moment," said Dr. Ellison. "Let's get you moving first, alright?"

"What happened to my body?" croaked Alex.

Dr. Ellison exchanged a glance with Sam before answering.

"Look Alex, I won't be able to give you good news on that front. You have been the target of a car bomb."

Dr. Ellison took a deep breath. "So your body has been impacted severely. But thanks to some very advanced medical technology, we could help you."

"Will I... be able... to walk... again?" asked Alex.

"Yes Alex, you will be able to walk again."

This relaxed Alex a bit. So it couldn't be that bad.

Then he noticed something else. "I am.. not breathing..."

"That's correct Alex, you are currently connected to a system that provides you with oxygenated fluids. Your brain is getting everything it needs. Don't worry about it for now."

"Alex," Sam took over again, "let's try to move your left arm now."

Alex tried to process everything that was happening, but it was impossible.

Maybe he was just dreaming and he would wake up soon. Until then he would follow their instructions.

So Alex did the same as before. First try nothing, second try success.

However he couldn't move the arm much, something was limiting the movement.

They repeated the process for his fingers and legs.

First try nothing, second try success. Yet every movement was limited and met with the same mechanical sound.

"Okay Alex," Dr. Ellison said, "the last muscle group we need to calibrate is your head and torso."

Calibrate? That was a weird word, thought Alex.

"Let's start with your neck. Please try to turn your head to the left."

Alex tried to turn his head left, but nothing happened as usual.

"Alright, try again Alex."

Alex focused and tried to turn his head again. This time, his head turned left. But there it was again, the weird mechanical sound. Even louder this time.

Dark thoughts started to creep into Alex' mind. He tried to focus, keeping them at bay.

"Okay Alex, before we remove all limitations, I need to tell you something very important," said Dr. Ellison.

Alex looked at the ceiling, bracing for impact.

"What is it?" asked Alex, the rhythm of his voice getting more confident.

"The most important thing is that you are alive. Okay? You will be able to be with your family and live a relatively normal life again."

Dr. Ellison took another deep breath.

"However, most of your body was damaged beyond repair and had to be replaced with prosthetics."

A wave of nausea washed over Alex. He knew it, his legs were gone. His arms probably too. Fuck!

"What is left?" asked Alex closing his eyes.

He was surprised he wasn't tearing up. Probably just another thing that wasn't working anymore, he thought bitterly.

Dr. Ellison nodded at Sam. "We will remove all restrictions now and allow you to move freely. Please take a look."

Alex opened his eyes and moved his arm in front of his face.

His hands and forearms were much larger, clad in black polycarbonate, with intricate mechanical joints visible at the seams.

His upper arms were silver metal, sleek and powerful, but devoid of any human warmth.

He lifted his right leg, revealing a similar design. The thigh and lower leg was a robust metal casing, while the knee was black polycarbonate again.

"Oh my god," Alex breathed, taking in the full extent of his transformation.

"Again Alex, you are the first human ever to have survived such an attack."

"What is left?" asked Alex again.

"You will be able to watch movies, play games, walk in the park with your family. Things won't be as easy as before, but you will be able to live a good life again."

"What is left?" shouted Alex, triggering Sam to quickly adjust some settings.

"Tell me!" he shouted with reduced volume.

"The only thing we can't replace: Your brain, Alex."
