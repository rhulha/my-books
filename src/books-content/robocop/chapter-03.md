Chapter 3 - Waking up

They walked barefoot along the shore in San Vito Lo Capo.
The air and sand was still warm.
The moon and a couple of beach lanterns painted shadows of their legs.
He drew Ellen closer. 

"Mr. Murphy, can you hear me?"

"Mr. Murphy, can you hear me? Ah now."

"Don't worry Mr. Murphy, you don't need to say anything. I can see if you hear me on my monitor."

"My name is Samantha and I will help you move again, okay? I'm here with Dr. Ellison."

"Hello Alex, everything is alright, just try to relax and follow Sam's instructions," said Dr. Ellison.

Then he nodded at Sam to continue.

"Let's start with your eye lids. Can you try to open them for me? Don't worry if it doesn't work right away. It will in a moment."

"Great, I have a reading. Give me a... moment... got it! Please try again."

Alex tried to open his eyes again. This time it worked.

A flush of light filled his vision. He blinked a couple of times to adjust.

He saw what appeared to be a white ceiling.

"Good, you're doing great Alex," said Sam. "Now, can you try to move your eyes a bit?"

"Again, don't worry if it doesn't work right away."

Alex tried to move his eyes, but it didn't work. Why couldn't he move his eyes? What the hell was going on?

He tried to move his arms and body, anything, but nothing happened.

"Please relax Mr. Murphy, you're doing great," said Sam soothingly.

"Everything will work very soon, I promise, we just have to take things step by step, okay?"

"Perfect! Now, can you follow my finger with your eyes?"

He moved his eyes as instructed, feeling more in control.

"Excellent! You're making progress," said Sam.

"Alright Alex, let's try to move your arms next," said Dr. Ellison.

Alex felt a surge of determination. He was ready to move again.
